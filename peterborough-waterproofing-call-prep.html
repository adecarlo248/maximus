Step 1 — Discovery and Workflow Map

Goal: understand how Peterborough Waterproofing currently runs.

What Tony needs to learn:

  • What happens when a new customer calls.
  • Who answers during business hours.
  • What happens after hours.
  • How estimates are booked.
  • What information is needed before an estimate.
  • Who handles follow-up.
  • Where leads currently get tracked.
  • Which admin tasks are slowing Justin and Belinda down.

Step 2 — Decide the Phone Number Path

There are two clean setup paths.

Path A — Automate their existing business number

Best if they want to keep using the current public business number as the main number customers already know.

Timeline: usually 2–3 weeks before the number is fully ready inside LeadConnector/HighLevel.

Why it takes longer:

  • The number has to be checked for eligibility.
  • Porting or carrier setup has to be completed.
  • Business SMS registration may be required.
  • Call routing has to be tested before going live.

Plain English explanation:

“If you want the current business number automated, that can absolutely be done, but it is not instant. The number has to be moved or connected properly so calls and texts can run through the system. That usually puts us in the two-to-three-week range before it is fully live.”

Path B — Use a new dedicated business automation number

Best if they want the fastest setup and want to separate personal, admin, and business communication.

Timeline: works as soon as setup is finished, usually within a few business days depending on how much automation they want.

Why this is faster:

  • Tony can provision the number directly inside the system.
  • Calls, texts, missed-call follow-up, and booking flows can be tested right away.
  • It keeps business communication separate from personal phones.
  • The old number can still exist while the new number handles automation.

Plain English explanation:

“The fastest route is I get you a new dedicated business number. That number can be connected to the system right away, and it separates business calls and texts from personal phones. You can still keep your current number, but this gives the automation its own lane.”

Step 3 — Build the Core System

Once the path is chosen, Tony builds the foundation:

  • Business phone number setup.
  • Missed-call text-back.
  • Booking calendar or estimate request flow.
  • Appointment confirmations.
  • Appointment reminders.
  • Lead follow-up messages.
  • Contact records and conversation history.
  • Mobile app access for the people who need it.
  • Basic pipeline or lead stages if useful.

Step 4 — Test Before Launch

Before anything goes live, test the real customer experience:

  • Test missed calls.
  • Test text replies.
  • Test booking flow.
  • Test appointment reminders.
  • Test notifications to Belinda/Justin.
  • Confirm the wording sounds like Peterborough Waterproofing.
  • Confirm the team knows where messages and appointments show up.

Step 5 — Go Live

Once approved, the system starts handling real leads.

The goal is a controlled launch, not chaos:

  • Start with the most important flow first.
  • Watch the first leads closely.
  • Fix small friction points quickly.
  • Keep the system simple enough that the team actually uses it.

Step 6 — Optimize After Real Use

After launch, Tony reviews what is happening and tightens the system.

Examples:

  • Adjust message wording.
  • Improve lead questions.
  • Add follow-up steps.
  • Add review requests.
  • Add invoice/payment workflows if needed.
  • Add more automation only after the basics are working.

What They Can Do From the App

They can:

  • See customer contact details.

What They Cannot Really Build From the App

They usually cannot build the deeper setup from mobile.

Desktop is needed for:

  • Building workflows and automations.
  • Creating pipelines and stages.
  • Setting up calendars.
  • Creating custom fields.
  • Connecting Stripe.
  • Setting up phone numbers and A2P registration. A2P means “Application-to-Person” messaging. In plain English, it is the business texting approval process that helps carriers verify the business and reduce spam so automated texts are more likely to deliver properly.
  • Building websites, funnels, forms, and email campaigns.
  • Managing advanced integrations.

FAQ for Tony’s Business Solutions

What exactly do you do?

We help local businesses automate the repetitive parts of customer communication — missed calls, text replies, appointment booking, reminders, follow-up, reviews, and basic lead management.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot is one piece. The bigger value is the full system behind it: calls, texts, booking, reminders, follow-up, reviews, invoices, and organized customer records.

Will it replace our staff?

No. It supports your staff. It handles repetitive tasks so your team can spend more time on real customer service and actual work.

Can it answer calls?

Yes, if they want that as part of the setup. It can answer calls, collect basic information, answer common questions, and help route the customer toward an estimate or callback.

Can it text customers automatically?

Yes. It can respond to missed calls, send confirmations, send appointment reminders, and follow up with leads who have not booked yet.

Can it book appointments?

Yes. Once the calendar is set up, the system can guide people toward booking an estimate or appointment.

Can we still answer manually?

Yes. You stay in control. The system helps when you are busy, after hours, or when follow-up needs to happen automatically.

Will customers know it is automated?

That depends how you want it positioned. The goal is to make it clear, helpful, and professional — not fake or confusing.

What if the system does not know the answer?

It should not guess. It can collect the customer’s information and let them know someone from the team will follow up.

Can this work from our current phone number?

Yes, but automating the current business number usually takes longer because the number has to be connected properly. The faster option is using a new dedicated business automation number.

Do we need to be tech-savvy?

No. Tony sets up the system. The team mostly uses the mobile app to respond, check appointments, make calls, and manage daily activity.

How long does setup take?

A new dedicated number setup can usually be live within a few business days after Tony has the required details. Automating an existing business number is usually a 2–3 week process.

What businesses is this best for?

Local service businesses that depend on calls, estimates, appointments, and fast follow-up — contractors, waterproofing, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, electricians, and similar trades.
