## Correct Payment Structure

### Level 1 — Referral Associate
Best for people who can make warm introductions but do not want to run sales calls.

Compensation:
- Standard client: $50 referral bonus
- Growth client: $100 referral bonus
- Revenue Engine client: $150 referral bonus
- No recurring commission at this level

### Level 2 — Sales Associate
Best for people who can prospect, qualify, follow up, and help close using the approved 1APP sales process.

Compensation:
- 15% of setup fee collected
- 20% of monthly recurring revenue for up to 12 paid months per client

### Level 3 — Certified 1APP Partner
Best for stronger sellers approved by Tony/Justin who can close cleanly and help protect the client relationship.

Compensation:
- 20% of setup fee collected
- 20% of monthly recurring revenue for the life of the client
- 5% override on setup + monthly recurring from associates personally recruited by the Certified Partner
- One level deep only — no override on recruits’ recruits
