1APP Sales Associate Email Copy/Paste Guide

Simple outreach copy for local businesses. Use this when introducing 1APP to owners who are missing leads, struggling with follow-up, or juggling too many tools.

Positioning

Plain-English pitch: 1APP helps businesses capture more leads, respond faster, book more appointments, follow up automatically, and keep everything organized in one place.

Best prospects: trades, home services, clinics, salons, real estate, local service businesses, consultants, and any business that depends on calls, forms, DMs, reviews, and appointments.

Pain Points to Mention

  • Missed calls turn into missed revenue because most customers call the next business if nobody answers.
  • Leads come from too many places: phone, website, Facebook, Instagram, Google, text, email, and referrals.
  • Follow-up gets forgotten when the owner or staff are busy.
  • Customers forget appointments unless reminders are sent.
  • Reviews are not requested consistently, even though reviews help win more local trust.
  • Owners are paying for multiple tools that do not talk to each other.

Selling Points

  • Speed-to-lead: Automatically responds when someone calls, texts, fills out a form, or books.
  • Missed Call Text Back: If a call is missed, the customer gets a text right away so the lead does not disappear.
  • Online booking: Customers can book without back-and-forth messages.
  • Automated reminders: Helps reduce no-shows and keeps customers informed.
  • Lead tracking: Keeps leads and conversations organized in one place.
  • Review requests: Helps businesses collect more Google reviews after jobs or appointments.
  • Simple setup: 1APP helps set up the system so the business does not have to figure it out alone.

Main Copy/Paste Email

Replace anything in brackets before sending.

Subject: Quick idea for [Business Name] Hi [First Name], I wanted to reach out because a lot of local businesses are losing leads without realizing it. When someone calls and nobody answers, fills out a form and does not get a fast reply, or tries to book and has to wait, they usually move on to the next business. That is the problem 1APP helps solve. 1APP gives businesses a simple system to: - Respond faster to new leads - Text back missed calls automatically - Let customers book appointments online - Send appointment confirmations and reminders - Keep leads and conversations organized - Follow up with prospects automatically - Request reviews from happy customers The goal is simple: help [Business Name] stop letting good leads slip through the cracks. It is especially useful for businesses that depend on calls, bookings, quotes, estimates, appointments, and repeat follow-up. Would you be open to a quick 10-minute conversation this week so I can show you how it works and see if it would help your business? Thanks, [Your Name] 1APP

Hey [First Name], quick one — 1APP helps businesses stop losing leads from missed calls, slow follow-up, and messy booking. It can text back missed calls, book appointments, send reminders, organize leads, and follow up automatically. Worth a quick 10-minute look this week?

The reason I reached out is because most businesses do not have a lead problem — they have a speed and follow-up problem. If a potential customer calls, messages, or submits a form and does not hear back fast, that opportunity can be gone. 1APP helps fix that by making sure leads are captured, followed up with, reminded, booked, and organized in one place. If you are open to it, I can show you the system in a quick 10-minute conversation and you can decide if it fits.

Simple Talk Track for Sales Associates

Use this if the prospect asks, “What is 1APP?”

“1APP is a business automation system that helps local businesses capture leads, respond faster, book appointments, follow up automatically, and keep everything organized. It is built for owners who are busy and do not want leads falling through the cracks.”

Best Call-to-Action

Do not over-explain in the first email. The goal is not to close the sale by email. The goal is to get a short conversation.

  • “Would you be open to a quick 10-minute conversation this week?”
  • “Can I show you how it works and see if it would help?”
  • “Worth a quick look?”
