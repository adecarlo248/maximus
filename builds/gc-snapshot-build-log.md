**Do NOT build this as a workflow.** Use GHL's built-in MCTB feature instead.

**How to enable:**
1. Go to **Settings → Phone Numbers**
2. Click on your phone number
3. Click the **"Voicemail & Missed Call TextBack"** tab
4. Check **"Enable Missed call textback"** ✅
5. Click **"Customize"** and paste the message below

**Customized message for this trade:**
"Hi {{contact.firstName}}, this is {{location.name}}! Sorry we missed your call — looking to discuss a renovation or construction project? Reply YES and we'll reach out right away 👋"

This fires automatically on every missed call. No workflow required.

### Workflow 1: New Lead — Speed to Response

**Trigger:** Opportunity created (filter: any GC pipeline, Stage: New Lead) OR Contact tag (filter: tag added = new-lead)
**Goal:** Response within 5 minutes — beat the competition

**Actions:**
1. **Immediate — SMS to Contact:**
   > Hey {{contact.first_name}}! This is [Company Name] — thanks for reaching out. We just got your info and someone from our team will be calling you in the next few minutes. If you need us right away, call or text this number. Talk soon!

2. **Immediate — Internal Task (assigned to PM/Owner):**
   > 🔔 NEW LEAD: {{contact.full_name}} | Phone: {{contact.phone}} | Source: {{contact.source}} | Call within 5 minutes!

3. **Wait 5 minutes — If no contact attempt logged:**

4. **Internal SMS/Notification to Owner:**
   > ⚠️ Speed to lead alert — {{contact.first_name}} has NOT been called yet (5 min passed). Source: {{contact.source}}. Call NOW: {{contact.phone}}
