**Core concept — Revolving vs. Fixed Debt:**

## Financial Concepts Primerica Teaches

**"Buy Term and Invest the Difference"**
- Don't buy expensive whole/universal life
- Buy affordable term for protection
- Invest the premium savings for wealth building

**Debt Elimination**
- Identify and eliminate revolving debt (credit cards)
- Consolidate into fixed payments where possible
- Redirect freed cash flow to savings

**The Debt-Free to Wealthy Path (illustrative example):**
- Person age 33, refinances $210,601 at 3.5% over 25 years
- Monthly payment: $1,051 (saves $1,516/month vs before)
- Takes $758/month and invests it at 9% for 12 years = $196,810
- At age 65 with continued investment: potential $2.91 million

**Retirement/Investment Principles:**
- Start small — even $25/month matters
- Use tax-advantaged accounts (RRSP, TFSA)
- Time in the market beats timing the market
- Financial advice = 3x more wealth over 15 years (research-backed)
