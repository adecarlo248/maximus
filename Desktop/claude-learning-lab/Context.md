# Current Project

## What I'm Building
I'm learning Claude and agentic AI specifically to scale my Primerica business faster.
The goal is to use AI as a force multiplier — so I can recruit more, follow up better,
run sharper presentations, and build a team that duplicates my systems.

## My Primerica Business Goals
- Build a strong warm market pipeline (100+ contacts actively worked)
- Recruit 2–4 new reps per month consistently
- Run 10+ FNAs per week
- Promote to RVP as fast as possible
- Help my team members do the same (duplication)

## How AI Fits In
- **Recruiting:** Draft outreach messages, texts, social posts to attract prospects
- **Follow-up:** Build follow-up scripts and sequences so no lead goes cold
- **FNA Prep:** Summarize client situations and generate talking points before meetings
- **Objection Handling:** Practice responses to common objections (cost, timing, "I need to think about it")
- **Team Training:** Create simple training materials and scripts my downline can use
- **Mindset & Motivation:** Help me stay sharp and prepared when momentum dips

## What Success Looks Like
- I have a library of AI-assisted scripts and templates I use daily
- I can brief Claude quickly and get a useful draft in under 2 minutes
- My follow-up is consistent because AI helps me stay organized
- My recruits have plug-and-play resources I built with AI
- I understand enough about agentic AI to automate repetitive tasks over time
