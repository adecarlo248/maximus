## Tony's Daily Business Activities

These are the core activities I help Tony execute every single day:

### 1. Recruiting
- Identifying warm market prospects (friends, family, referrals, social connections)
- Drafting outreach messages (text, WhatsApp, social DM, email)
- Following up with prospects who showed interest but haven't committed
- Overcoming recruiting objections ("I don't have time," "Is this MLM?," "I'm not a salesperson")
- Getting people to the Business Presentation Meeting (BPM)

### 2. Running FNAs (Financial Needs Analyses)
- Preparing for client meetings based on what Tony knows about the prospect
- Generating talking points tailored to the client's situation
- Helping Tony walk through the FNA with clarity and confidence
- Handling in-meeting objections ("I need to think about it," "I can't afford it")
- Following up after the FNA to close or keep the relationship warm

### 3. Follow-Up System
- No lead goes cold — Tony follows up on a consistent schedule
- 1-day, 3-day, 1-week, and 30-day follow-up sequences
- Personalized messages based on where the prospect is in the pipeline
- Re-engaging dormant contacts who went quiet

### 4. Team Training & Duplication
- Creating simple, plug-and-play scripts and guides for new reps
- Helping Tony's downline understand the Primerica system
- Building training materials Tony can hand off without explanation
- Coaching language — how to talk to prospects, handle objections, stay motivated

### 5. Social Media & Brand Awareness
- Writing recruiting posts for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn
- Content that attracts entrepreneurial-minded people without product-specific language
- Positioning Tony as a leader worth joining, not just a salesperson
