## 1APP Revenue Reactivation Sprint — verified-estimate tracking

- Tony asked to preserve the recommended billing-proof workflow for future Sprint clients.
- Do not bill from a booking or from the client's verbal claim that an estimate was sent.
- Use a two-step control: the client logs the written estimate and evidence; 1APP reviews it and marks it **Verified** before it becomes billable.
- Recommended operational stages: Outreach Sent → Interested Reply → Qualified → Appointment Booked → Estimate Pending (appointment kept) → Estimate Issued (awaiting verification) → Verified Estimate (billable) → Excluded/Closed.
- Required fields: Sprint campaign/cohort ID, customer/contact ID, estimate number, sent date/time, pre-tax value, project category, estimator, protected evidence reference, verification status/by/date, exclusion or dispute reason, agreed fee, remaining campaign ceiling, and invoice status.
- A billable verified estimate must come from the approved cohort and attribution window, follow a kept appointment, meet the written service/geography/minimum-value rules, and have evidence accepted by 1APP.
- Exclude bookings alone, no-shows/cancellations, verbal ballparks, pre-existing jobs or estimates, duplicates/reissues/revisions, change orders, excluded small service calls, and anything outside the approved cohort or rules.
- First-launch capture method: secure internal **Log an Estimate** form in the client sub-account. Submission moves the opportunity to Estimate Issued and alerts 1APP. Later, integrate the client's estimating software, but retain manual verification until proven reliable.
- Reconcile weekly. Show estimate number, contact ID/initials, sent date, value, fee, and verified/excluded/disputed status. Proposed dispute window: five business days. Revisions to one estimate number never create another fee. Pause automatically when the agreed estimate count or fee ceiling is reached.
- Contract concept: “An estimate becomes billable only after the client issues a written estimate meeting Schedule A's qualification rules and provides verifiable evidence accepted by 1APP.”
- Riley-specific proposal remains unapproved: up to five verified estimates / CAD $2,500 success-fee ceiling if CAD $500 per estimate is supported and approved.
