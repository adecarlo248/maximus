## QuickBooks / Make — Estimate-to-Invoice Automation
- Tony confirmed the client can manually convert accepted QuickBooks estimates to invoices through a button, but the actual service value is time savings: automate it.
- Desired workflow: when a customer accepts a QuickBooks estimate, Make.com automatically creates/converts the invoice and sends it to the customer.
- Recommended Make scenario: QuickBooks New Event / updated estimate trigger → filter for accepted/closed estimate status → retrieve full estimate details → create invoice mapped from estimate/customer/line items → send invoice email → mark/log processed to prevent duplicates.
- Key build warning: verify QuickBooks' exact status/value after customer acceptance in the live account before finalizing the filter.
