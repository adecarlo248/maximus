## Peterborough Waterproofing — QuickBooks e-Transfer Automation
- Tony is exploring how to automate Peterborough Waterproofing e-transfer payment emails into QuickBooks without pushing the client fully to Stripe/payment processor fees.
- Recommendation direction: QBO bank feed/rules are the simplest low-cost layer for deposit capture, but Make.com + Parseur/Mailparser is still the best workaround if the goal is to automatically match e-transfer emails to open QBO invoices, mark invoices paid, update GHL, and route exceptions to the mobile-only owner.
- Key framing: GHL remains the client-facing dashboard; QBO is the accounting source of truth; Make/Parseur are backend plumbing Tony manages.
