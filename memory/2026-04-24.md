### Technical Notes
- Uses Playwright (headless Chromium) — NOT Selenium (snap conflict on WSL2)
- FSRA site is ASP.NET postback — no navigation events, DOM polling required
- Two-pass approach: Pass 1 collects hrefs via pagination, Pass 2 visits ShowAgent.aspx directly
- Phone lookup: 411.ca primary, Canada411 fallback
- All 130 agents returned phone numbers (quality varies — local 705/613 most reliable)
