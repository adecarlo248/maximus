Access model recorded in the hiring/training pack:
- Sales Manager receives a dedicated **Sales Manager — Corporate Sales** standard-user role, not administrator access.
- Manager can manage corporate prospects, sales/recruiting pipelines, conversations, tasks, demo calendars, approved quotes, team coaching, and reporting.
- Billing, bank/tax data, users/roles, integrations, domains, phone administration, live automations, exports, bulk deletion, and company administration remain blocked.
