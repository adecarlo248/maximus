Workflows that make sense for KCIP

1. Missed-call text back

When a call is missed, KCIP instantly texts: “Thanks for calling KCIP — are you looking for pool service, liner/revamp, new pool install, concrete/turf, or landscaping?”

Result: fewer lost leads while the crew is on job sites.

2. AI Voice receptionist

Answers overflow and after-hours calls, qualifies the job, captures details, and routes urgent pool issues differently from major backyard projects.

Result: Scott stops being the bottleneck for first response.

3. Estimate booking calendar

Replace vague “Free Quote” with “Book Your Backyard Estimate” and controlled estimate windows.

Result: less phone tag, more booked consultations.

4. Quote follow-up

After an estimate, unclosed quotes get smart follow-up at 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, and 14 days with proof, photos, and easy reply options.

Result: more quotes turn into deposits.

5. Seasonal reminders

Spring openings, inspections, leak checks, fall closings, liner replacement reminders, and “book next season early” campaigns.

Result: repeat revenue without manual chasing.

6. Review + referral engine

Completed jobs trigger review requests, referral prompts, and private save paths for unhappy customers.

Result: stronger local proof against bigger competitors.
