## What the Customer Experience Can Look Like

### 1. A Customer Calls

A customer calls Peterborough Waterproofing because they need help with a waterproofing, foundation, basement, or moisture issue.

If the team answers, the call works like normal.

If the call is missed, the system can automatically send a professional text response within seconds.

Example:

“Hi, thanks for calling Peterborough Waterproofing. Sorry we missed you. Are you looking to book an estimate or speak with someone about a waterproofing issue?”

### 2. The Customer Responds

The system can collect basic details such as:

- Name
- Phone number
- Address or service area
- Type of issue
- Best time to follow up
- Whether they want to request an estimate

### 3. The Lead Gets Organized

Instead of the lead living only in someone’s call log or text messages, it can be saved into a simple contact record.

The team can see:

- Who contacted the business
- What they need help with
- Whether they were contacted
- Whether an estimate was booked
- What follow-up is needed next

### 4. Estimate Booking or Callback

Depending on how Peterborough Waterproofing wants to operate, the system can either:

- Guide the customer toward booking an estimate, or
- Collect the information and notify the team to follow up personally.

This gives the business control over how automated or hands-on the process should be.

### 5. Appointment Confirmations and Reminders

Once an estimate or appointment is booked, the system can send:

- Confirmation messages
- Reminder messages
- Follow-up messages if needed

This helps reduce forgotten appointments and keeps customers informed.

### 6. Follow-Up and Reviews

After the job or estimate process, the system can support follow-up and review requests.

Examples:

- Follow up with leads who have not booked yet.
- Follow up after an estimate.
- Ask happy customers for a Google review.
- Keep customer communication organized for future reference.

## What a Pipeline Is

A pipeline is a simple visual tracker that shows where each lead or customer is in the process.

Example stages for Peterborough Waterproofing could be:

1. New Lead
2. Contacted
3. Estimate Booked
4. Estimate Completed
5. Quote Sent
6. Job Won
7. Job Lost
8. Follow-Up Needed

It works like moving cards from one stage to the next.

This helps the team quickly see:

- Who needs a callback
- Who has an estimate booked
- Who is waiting on a quote
- Who needs follow-up
- Which opportunities are active

The main value is visibility. Nothing has to live only in memory, voicemail, or scattered text threads.

## Two Setup Paths for the Phone Number

There are two clean ways to set up the phone system.

### Option 1 — Automate the Existing Business Number

This is best if Peterborough Waterproofing wants customers to keep using the current public business number.

Typical timeline: **2–3 weeks**

Why it takes longer:

- The number needs to be checked for eligibility.
- The number may need to be connected or ported.
- Business texting registration may be required.
- Call routing and text delivery need to be tested before launch.

This path is a good fit if keeping the current number as the main number is the priority.

### Option 2 — Use a New Dedicated Business Automation Number

This is the fastest option.

Typical timeline: **3–7 business days after required details are collected**

Why it is faster:

- A new number can be set up directly inside the system.
- Calls and texts can be tested quickly.
- It separates business communication from personal phones.
- The current business number can still stay in place while the new number handles automation.

This path is a good fit if speed, separation, and clean organization are the priority.

## What A2P Means in Plain English

A2P means “Application-to-Person” messaging.

In plain English, it is the business texting approval process carriers use to verify that a real business is sending legitimate messages.

It helps reduce spam and improves the chances that automated business texts are delivered properly.

This may be required when a business wants to send automated text messages through the system.
