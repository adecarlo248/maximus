The Breakdown
5 Ways Trades Businesses Leak Revenue Every Single Day
Problem 01

Missed Calls With No Follow-Up

When a customer calls and gets voicemail, 85% do not leave a message. They hang up and call the next contractor. If you're not texting back within 5 minutes, that lead is gone. Most trades businesses have no automatic follow-up at all — it relies on the owner remembering to call back between jobs.

Problem 02

No-Shows Eating Your Schedule

No-shows cost the average trades business 6–10 hours of lost time per month. Without automated reminders — 24 hours and 1 hour before the appointment — customers forget, reschedule last minute, or simply don't show. That's billable time you'll never get back.

Problem 03

Leads That Go Cold Before You Call Back

You get busy. You intend to call back. Three days later you find the number. By then the customer has already hired someone else and forgotten they even called you. Most trades businesses have no system for following up — they rely entirely on memory. Memory is not a system.

Problem 04

Zero Google Reviews Despite Doing Great Work

You finish a great job. The customer is happy. You move on to the next one. Nobody asks for a review, and the customer never thinks to leave one. Meanwhile, your competitor down the road has 140 reviews because he sends an automatic review request text 30 minutes after every job. That's the difference between page 1 and page 3 on Google.

Problem 05

After-Hours Calls Going Straight to Voicemail

A homeowner's hot water tank dies at 9pm on a Sunday. They call three plumbers. Two go to voicemail. One has an AI that answers, asks about the issue, and books an emergency appointment for 7am Monday. Which business gets the call? After-hours availability used to be a differentiator. In 2026, customers expect it.
