## What Is a Shadow Operator?

A **Shadow Operator** is the invisible business partner behind a creator — you build, run, and scale their digital (or physical) product business while they focus on content and audience. The creator is the face. You are the engine.

Think of it like a music producer vs the artist. The artist performs. The producer builds the hit, takes a cut, and moves to the next one.

The concept lives inside Iman Gadzhi's **Monetise** program and leverages **Whop** as the distribution/hosting platform.

**Core premise:** Creators have audiences but no systems. You have systems but no audience. Match them. Split the revenue. Scale.

## The Shadow Operator Model — How It Works

### Step 1: Find the Right Creator
Target creators who have:
- **10K–500K followers** (sweet spot — big enough to matter, small enough to not have a team yet)
- **An engaged audience** (comments, replies, DMs — not ghost followers)
- **No existing digital product** or a weak one
- **Expertise, story, or lifestyle** that their audience wants to learn from

Platforms to source creators:
- Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Substack
- Niche-specific: fitness, finance, relationships, productivity, faith, cooking, real estate, business

### Step 2: Identify the Product Opportunity
What does this audience want to BUY from this creator?

Common digital product types:
- **Ebooks / PDF guides** — low barrier, easy to create with AI
- **Mini courses / video courses** — higher ticket, higher perceived value
- **Community memberships** — recurring revenue, high LTV
- **Templates / swipe files / toolkits** — quick wins for buyers
- **Coaching / consulting programs** — highest ticket, most hands-on
- **Physical products / merch** — brand extension, higher logistics but loyal buyers

### Step 3: The Pitch to the Creator
You're not asking for a job. You're offering a business partnership.

**Your pitch framework:**
- "I build and run digital product businesses for creators"
- "You keep 100% focus on your content — I handle everything else"
- "We split revenue [typically 20–50% depending on deal structure and your contribution]"
- "Zero upfront cost to you — I only eat when you eat"

**What you're offering to handle:**
- Product creation (using AI tools from Monetise / Synthesise)
- Whop store setup and optimization
- Pricing strategy
- Sales funnel / landing page
- Email follow-up sequences
- Customer support systems
- Analytics and scaling

### Step 4: Build the Product (Using AI)
Monetise includes AI tools (Synthesise) that dramatically accelerate product creation:

- **AI Ghostwriter OS** — writes ebooks, guides, scripts in the creator's voice
- **AI Course Builder** — structures curriculum and lesson content
- **Template Generators** — builds swipe files and toolkits fast
- **Sales Page Copy** — writes high-converting product pages

The "10-80-10 Rule" mentioned in Monetise reviews:
- 10% = your creative direction
- 80% = AI does the heavy lifting
- 10% = your final polish

### Step 5: Host Everything on Whop
Whop is the platform of choice because:
- Handles payments, subscriptions, one-time products
- Built-in community features (chat, forums)
- Drip content / course delivery
- Digital file delivery
- Affiliate program management
- Analytics dashboard
- Low fees vs competitors (Kajabi, Teachable, Gumroad)
- Iman is literally a co-owner = deep integration with Monetise ecosystem

**Product types Whop supports:**
- Digital downloads (PDFs, templates, files)
- Online courses (video, drip content)
- Memberships / communities
- 1:1 coaching bookings
- Software / app access
- Physical product pre-orders (with integrations)

### Step 6: Drive Sales
You handle the traffic and conversion strategy:

**Organic (leverage creator's existing audience):**
- Launch announcement to their existing followers
- Stories/posts driving to Whop link
- Email list blast (if they have one)
- YouTube description / pinned comment
- Bio link update

**Paid (scale what's working):**
- Meta ads targeting the creator's niche
- Retargeting warm audiences
- Whop's built-in affiliate tools (recruit buyers to promote)

**Community-driven:**
- Build testimonials fast (launch offer / early bird pricing)
- Use Whop community features to create social proof loops

## Revenue Split Models

| Your Contribution | Creator Contribution | Your Cut |
|---|---|---|
| Build + Run everything | Audience + Face | 40–50% |
| Build product + setup | Run the marketing | 25–35% |
| Consulting + oversight | Their team runs ops | 10–20% |

**Rule of thumb:** The more you do, the more you take. Start around 30–40% for a full-service deal.
