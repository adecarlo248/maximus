- **Panel upgrade demand** is surging as homes built in the 1970s–1990s hit 25–40 year electrical service lifespans and new appliance loads (EV, heat pumps, induction stoves) push 100A panels to capacity

## Section 1: Electrical Business Pain Points

### 1.1 Operations Pain Points

**After-Hours Emergency Calls — The Silent Revenue Drain**
- A missed emergency call at 10pm goes to the next guy in Google. That's a $500–$1,200 lost job.
- Most electrical shops have zero automated response system. The phone rings, nobody answers, the homeowner calls the next contractor.
- Emergency dispatch automation is one of the most immediate ROI wins a GHL snapshot can deliver.

**No-Shows and Quote Ghost**
- Electricians book estimates, customers don't show. Industry average no-show rate: 15–25%
- No confirmation sequence = 20% of booked quotes don't happen
- No reminder before the appointment = customer forgets, contractor wastes a $75–150 drive time
- No post-no-show follow-up = lead goes permanently cold

**Permit Delays and Inspection Scheduling Chaos**
- Electrical permits are required for: panel upgrades, rewires, additions, EV charger circuits, new construction
- Permit processing time: 2–10 business days (varies by municipality)
- Inspection scheduling: another 3–10 business day delay after work is complete
- Most contractors have no system to communicate these delays to customers, leading to angry callbacks and 1-star reviews ("they disappeared after starting the work")
- A permit status update sequence is gold — nearly zero contractors have this

**Quoting Complexity and Low Conversion Rates**
- Electrical quotes require: site visit, load calculation assessment, material pricing, labor estimate, permit cost
- Electricians send a PDF quote by email, then never follow up
- Industry average quote-to-close rate: 35–50% for residential
- A proper quote follow-up sequence (Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14) can lift close rate to 55–65%

**EV Charger Lead Chaos**
- Google search volume for "EV charger installation" up 300% since 2022
- Electricians getting 10–30 EV charger inquiries/month with no system to pre-qualify or nurture
- Many EV charger prospects need education: rebate programs, Level 1 vs Level 2 vs Level 3, panel capacity, permit requirement
- Electricians spend 20–30 minutes on unqualified inbound calls that go nowhere
- An EV charger intake form + automated qualification sequence eliminates this bottleneck

**Panel Upgrade Opportunity — Most Missed Upsell in the Trade**
- Homes 25+ years old with 100A service panels are prime candidates for 200A service upgrade
- Panel upgrades are triggered by: new EV charger (often can't add 50A circuit without upgrade), hot tub install, new HVAC system, kitchen renovation, addition
- Average panel upgrade job: $3,500–$8,000 — highest margin residential electrical job
- Most electricians fail to proactively sell panel upgrades when doing smaller service calls
- A database reactivation campaign targeting past customers (service date 2+ years ago) in older homes is low-hanging revenue

**Review Generation Failure**
- Electrical contractors live and die by Google reviews
- Most have 15–40 reviews even after 10+ years in business
- Zero post-job review request automation = reviews trickle in at 1–3/month instead of 5–15/month
- Contractors with 100+ Google reviews get 3–5x the inbound calls of contractors with 20 reviews

**Referral Capture Vacuum**
- Homeowners who just had a good electrical experience are warm referral sources for 6 months
- Real estate agents, general contractors, renovation companies, property managers are repeat referral sources
- No contractor has a system to nurture these relationships after job completion

### 1.2 Sales and Marketing Pain Points

**Invisible Online Presence**
- Most electrical contractors have a 5-year-old website, zero social media, no Google Business Profile management
- They rely on word-of-mouth and HomeStars/Angi listings — zero owned lead generation
- Google LSA (Local Service Ads) is available for electricians and delivers pre-verified leads but requires fast response time — most contractors can't respond in under 5 minutes consistently

**No Seasonal or Proactive Outreach**
- Generator installs spike every fall (storm season awareness)
- EV charger inquiries spike every spring and when gas prices rise
- Panel upgrade awareness campaigns in spring/fall renovation season
- Surge protection package upsells in spring (storm/lightning season)
- No electrical contractor has a seasonal marketing calendar with automated campaigns

**Commercial/Property Manager Pipeline Zero**
- Property managers oversee 50–500 units each, needing periodic electrical inspections, emergency calls, and upgrades
- A single property manager relationship can be worth $20,000–$100,000/year
- Zero contractors have a systematic outreach and nurture sequence for this segment

## Section 2: GHL Electrical Snapshots — Market Assessment

### 2.1 What Currently Exists

The current GHL snapshot marketplace for electrical contractors is weak. Here's the honest assessment:

**Generic Home Services Snapshots** (what's available)
- Snapshots from agencies like "HL Pro Tools", "Extendly", and various creators exist for "trades" or "home services"
- These typically include: basic lead capture form, 2–3 SMS follow-up messages, appointment calendar, basic pipeline
- **What they miss:** electrical-specific language, permit workflow automation, EV charger sequences, panel upgrade nurture, emergency dispatch, commercial segment, load calculation intake, rebate education

**HVAC-Focused Snapshots** (closest adjacent)
- More mature than electrical — HVAC has had CRM adoption longer
- Include seasonal campaigns (cooling check, heating tune-up), maintenance plan workflows
- Can be adapted but lack electrical-specific elements entirely

**Plumbing Snapshots**
- Emergency-first structure (closest to emergency electrical model)
- Good emergency dispatch logic but no project-type branching for electrical (panel vs EV vs rewire vs service call)

**The Gap in Plain English:**
There is no GHL snapshot on the market that:
- Has dedicated EV charger consultation funnel
- Includes permit status communication automation
- Has panel upgrade nurture sequence targeting homes 25+ years old
- Differentiates between emergency service, planned service, project work, and commercial accounts
- Has electrician-specific SMS/email copy that sounds like a real electrician

This is the white space 1app owns.

### 2.2 Competitive Pricing

- Generic home services snapshots: $97–$197 one-time or included in $97–297/mo SaaS plans
- HVAC-specific snapshots: $197–$497 one-time

## Section 4: Workflow Architecture

### 4.1 Emergency Dispatch Workflow

**Trigger:** New lead with "Emergency" tag OR "No power", "burning smell", "sparking outlet", "tripped breaker won't reset", "power outage" keywords in form/message

**Step 1 — Immediate (0 min):**
- SMS to prospect: *"⚡ [Company Name] here — we got your emergency request. Our electrician is being dispatched. You'll receive a call with ETA within 5 minutes. If urgent, call us directly: [Phone]"*
- Internal notification to dispatcher/owner (SMS + email)
- Task created: "Call lead NOW — Emergency"

**Step 2 — If No Response (5 min):**
- Trigger auto-call (GHL AI voice) or manual call prompt

**Step 3 — Dispatch Confirmed:**
- SMS to customer: *"Your electrician [Name] is on the way. ETA approximately [X] minutes. He'll call when 10 minutes out."*

**Step 4 — Post-Job (24 hours):**
- SMS review request
- Tag for "Past Emergency Customer" — add to panel upgrade awareness campaign at 30 days

---

### 4.2 EV Charger Consultation Workflow

**Trigger:** EV Charger form submitted or "EV charger" / "electric car charger" / "Level 2 charger" in inquiry

**Step 1 — Immediate:**
- SMS: *"Got your EV charger request! We'll be in touch within 2 hours to book your free site assessment. While you wait — did you know you may qualify for a rebate of up to $700 on your EV charger installation? Check your eligibility: [Link to rebate info page]"*

**Step 2 — 30 min:**
- Email: EV Charger Education Package
  - Level 1 vs Level 2 vs Level 3 differences
  - What to expect during installation (dedicated 240V circuit, load calculation, permit)
  - Current rebate programs available in their province/state
  - Timeline from booking to charging: typically 1–3 weeks

**Step 3 — 2 hours (Business Hours):**
- Human call or AI voice: Book site assessment appointment

**Step 4 — Site Assessment Booked:**
- Confirmation SMS + email with appointment details
- 24-hour reminder SMS
- 2-hour reminder SMS

**Step 5 — Post-Assessment:**
- SMS same day: *"Thanks for having us out today! You'll receive your detailed quote for your Level 2 EV charger installation within 24 hours. Any questions, reply to this text."*

**Step 6 — Quote Sent:**
- Quote follow-up sequence (below)

**Step 7 — Job Complete:**
- SMS: *"Your Level 2 EV charger is live! 🔋 Need help with the rebate paperwork? We can send you the form — just reply YES."*
- Review request at 24 hours
- Referral ask at 7 days: *"Do you know anyone else who just got an EV or is thinking about it? We'd love to help them too — and we'll send you a [gift card/discount] for every referral."*

### 4.3 Panel Upgrade Nurture Sequence

**Trigger:** Panel upgrade inquiry OR "100A panel" tag during site visit OR home built before 2000 tag

**Day 0 — Email: Why Your Panel Might Need Upgrading**

- Email: *"Is your home ready for an EV charger or heat pump?"*

**Day 7 — Last Touch Before Monthly:**
- SMS: *"Just checking in — panel upgrades typically book out 2–3 weeks once we pull the permit. If you want this done before [season], it's worth booking your assessment this week. No pressure, just want to make sure you're not stuck waiting."*

**Day 14 — Email: Social Proof**

**Day 30, 60, 90 — Monthly Check-in:**

## Section 7: Booking Flow Architecture

### 7.1 Three Booking Types — Setup in GHL

**Booking Type 1: Emergency / Same-Day**
- No calendar required — goes to phone/SMS queue
- GHL form → immediate SMS to prospect + immediate internal alert
- Goal: human response within 5 minutes
- DO NOT use standard calendar for emergencies — creates wrong expectations

**Booking Type 2: Quote / Site Assessment (Scheduled)**
- Standard GHL calendar
- Calendar name: "Free Electrical Assessment"
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Buffer: 30 min after each appointment
- Confirmation: Immediate SMS + Email
- Reminder: 24-hour SMS + 2-hour SMS
- Post-appointment: Follow-up sequence triggers on "Appointment Completed"

**Booking Type 3: Installation Day**
- Separate calendar: "Installation Booking"
- Duration: Blocked time (half-day or full-day options)
- Requires prior quote acceptance (pipeline stage gate)
- Reminder: 48-hour email + 24-hour SMS + morning-of SMS
