## SECTION 1: INDUSTRY OVERVIEW & TERMINOLOGY

### Core Service Lines

| Service | Description | Avg Ticket (Residential) |
|---|---|---|
| **Gutter Cleaning** | Remove debris (leaves, twigs, pine needles, shingle grit) from K-style or half-round gutters; flush downspouts | $125–$320 |
| **Seamless Gutter Installation** | Custom-fabricated K-style or half-round aluminum/copper gutters cut on-site; typically priced per linear foot | $8–$20/linear foot installed |
| **Gutter Guard / Leaf Guard Installation** | Micro-mesh, reverse-curve, foam, or brush inserts that prevent debris accumulation | $6–$30/linear foot installed |
| **Downspout Repair / Replacement** | Re-attaching, replacing, or re-routing downspouts and extensions | $65–$175 per downspout |
| **Fascia & Soffit Repair** | Rotted or damaged wood/vinyl fascia board repair before new gutter installation | $500–$2,500+ depending on scope |
| **End Cap Replacement** | Sealing open gutter ends; common after storm damage | $25–$75 |
| **Spike & Hanger Replacement** | Re-securing gutters that have pulled away from fascia; spikes replaced with hangers | $3–$8 per hanger installed |
| **Slope / Pitch Correction** | Re-pitching gutters that have sagged or lost proper slope toward downspouts | $150–$400 |
| **Gutter Realignment** | Full re-hang of existing gutters for proper water flow | $175–$500 |

### Industry Vocabulary (Use Throughout All GHL Assets)

- **K-style gutter** — Most common residential profile; flat bottom, ogee-shaped face; handles more volume than half-round
- **Half-round gutter** — Traditional rounded profile; common on older/historic homes; premium pricing
- **Seamless gutter** — Fabricated on-site from coil stock; no seams = fewer leak points; highest quality install
- **Sectional gutter** — Pre-cut lengths connected with seam connectors; prone to leaks; less preferred
- **Gutter guard / leaf guard** — Generic term for any debris-blocking system
- **Micro-mesh guard** — Premium tier; stainless steel mesh blocks even fine debris
- **Reverse-curve / surface tension guard** — Water clings to curved surface, debris falls off
- **Foam guard** — Low cost, fills gutter interior; least effective; don't recommend
- **Downspout** — Vertical pipe carrying water from gutter to ground or drain
- **Downspout extension** — Horizontal piece directing water away from foundation
- **End cap** — Caps the open ends of gutter runs
- **Spike and hanger** — Fasteners holding gutter to fascia board; spikes (old) vs. hidden hangers (modern)
- **Fascia board** — Horizontal board behind gutter at roofline; must be solid for secure installation
- **Soffit** — Underside of roof overhang; ventilation and aesthetic panel
- **Linear feet** — Unit used to measure gutter runs for pricing and quoting
- **Pitch / slope** — Gradient of gutter toward downspout (typically 1/4" per 10 linear feet)
- **Elbow** — Angled connector for downspout routing around corners
- **Splash block** — Ground-level concrete or plastic block under downspout to direct water

## SECTION 2: BUSINESS PAIN POINTS — THE 8 PROBLEMS THIS SNAPSHOT SOLVES

### Pain Point 1: Speed-to-Lead Is Killing Conversions
Most gutter companies respond to estimate requests in 4–24+ hours. The national average time-to-response for home services leads is 47 hours. First responder wins the job 78% of the time. A homeowner who submits an estimate form at 8pm on Thursday is comparing 3 companies — whoever texts back first within 5 minutes owns the conversation.

**GHL Fix:** Instant SMS/email triggered on any new lead form submission. AI bot handles pre-qualification (single story or two-story? aluminum or existing copper? date flexibility?).

---

### Pain Point 2: Seasonal Revenue Cliffs
Gutter companies have two peak seasons: **Fall (October–November)** and **Spring (March–April)**. Off-season months — July, August, January, February — have near-zero organic demand. Most companies have no system to generate work during slow months or to pre-book the next season.

**GHL Fix:** Semi-annual cleaning reminder campaigns timed to Spring and Fall. Pre-book customers in August for October cleaning slots. Create urgency: "Our Fall schedule is 60% full — lock in your spot now."

---

### Pain Point 3: No Gutter Guard Upsell System
Gutter guard / leaf guard installation is the highest-margin job in the category. Margins run 60–80% on product + labor. A customer who installs micro-mesh guards eliminates cleaning revenue but is worth 3–5x the lifetime value in a single transaction ($1,500–$4,000+ for a full residential system) and generates massive referral activity. Most companies pitch guards verbally during a cleaning — and never follow up. If the homeowner says "let me think about it," the company never contacts them again.

**GHL Fix:** Automated 5-step gutter guard follow-up sequence triggered when a cleaning customer is tagged "guard_upsell_pending."

---

### Pain Point 4: Zero Review Capture
Reviews are the #1 sales tool for any home services company. Google Business reviews directly affect LSA (Local Services Ad) ranking and cost per lead. Most gutter companies have fewer than 20 reviews despite completing hundreds of jobs per year. The problem is simple: no one asks.

**GHL Fix:** Automated post-job review request SMS + email sent 2 hours after job completion tag. Second request at 48 hours if no review. Direct Google review link embedded.

---

### Pain Point 5: Storm Events Are Missed
After a major wind or hail event, gutter damage spikes. Gutters get bent, end caps pop, hangers fail, and debris volume goes from manageable to overflowing. This creates a 48–72 hour window of extreme demand. Companies with no outreach system miss 90% of this demand because they're already slammed with inbound calls and can't proactively reach their customer list.

**GHL Fix:** Storm campaign workflow — one-click blast to entire customer database. Storm-specific SMS template. Creates its own demand surge from existing customers.

---

### Pain Point 6: No Roofing Company Referral System
Roofing companies replace or repair gutters as a secondary service but almost always refer out to a trusted gutter company. A single roofing contractor can send 5–15 gutter jobs per month. Most gutter companies get referrals from 1–2 roofers via informal text messages with no tracking, no thank-you system, and no nurture.

**GHL Fix:** Dedicated roofing partner sub-pipeline. Referral tracking. Automated thank-you + reciprocal lead sharing invitation.

---

### Pain Point 7: No Recurring Revenue Mechanism
A gutter cleaning customer is worth $150–$320 per visit. Without a recurring program, they're a one-time transaction. With a semi-annual cleaning program ($250–$550/year), they become a predictable recurring revenue account. Most gutter companies offer this but never formally enroll customers or create any friction to cancel.

**GHL Fix:** Semi-Annual Program enrollment workflow. Digital program agreement. Automated biannual reminder sequence for enrolled customers.

---

### Pain Point 8: Post-Install Dead Zone
After a seamless gutter installation or gutter guard install, customers go completely dark. No warranty check-in. No referral request. No upsell to guard or cleaning. The post-installation relationship is the single highest-leverage moment for referrals — homeowners are excited, they're looking at their new gutters, they're talking to neighbors. Most companies do nothing.

**GHL Fix:** 3-touch post-install sequence: (1) 48-hour satisfaction check, (2) 7-day referral request, (3) 6-month check-in / cleaning upsell.
