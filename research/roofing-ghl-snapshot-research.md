## 1. ROOFING BUSINESS PAIN POINTS

### The Core Problems Roofing Companies Face

**Lead Chaos**
- Leads come in from 6–10 different sources simultaneously (Facebook, Google, door knock forms, referrals, storm chasing, insurance adjusters) with no unified inbox
- Most roofers respond to leads 4–24 hours later — industry average is 5.7 hours; conversion drops 80% if you wait more than 5 minutes
- No-shows and ghosting on estimate appointments are rampant (industry no-show rate: 20–30%)
- 80% of roofing sales go to the company that responds first AND follows up the most

**Revenue Leakage**
- No estimate follow-up system — reps call once, get voicemail, move on
- Contracts never get signed electronically — chasing paper kills velocity
- No referral capture system — most roofers get 30–50% of jobs from word of mouth but have zero automated referral asks
- Seasonality swings destroy cash flow — no off-season nurture campaigns

**Insurance Claim Complexity**
- 40–60% of residential roofing revenue in hail/storm states comes from insurance claims
- Contractors lose deals because homeowners don't know the claim process — nobody educates them
- Insurance adjuster meetings don't get scheduled systematically
- Supplement follow-up with insurance companies is manual and gets dropped

**Review Deficit**
- Google reviews are the #1 decision factor for roofing (homeowners pick companies with 4.8+ stars)
- Most companies have under 50 reviews — top companies in any market have 200–500+
- No system to request reviews at the right moment (right after job complete = highest conversion)
- Negative review management is nonexistent

**Operational Gaps**
- No pre-job communication (homeowners don't know when crew arrives, what to expect)
- No post-job follow-up to catch issues before they become reviews
- Storm chasing companies have no geo-targeted lead intake form
- Door knocking teams have no digital lead capture — still using paper

## 4. ROOFING-SPECIFIC WORKFLOWS

### Priority Automations (Build These First)

#### Workflow 1: Missed Call Text Back
**Trigger:** Incoming call not answered
**Sequence:**
- Immediate (0 min): "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, this is [Company Name]. Sorry we missed your call! Did you need a free roof inspection or estimate? Reply YES and we'll reach out right away."
- 5 min: If no reply → assign call task to rep
- 30 min: If no reply → "Still here if you need us! Most of our customers are shocked at what their roof looks like after a storm. We do free inspections. Want one?"

#### Workflow 2: New Lead — Speed to Lead (CRITICAL)
**Trigger:** Any new lead (Facebook ad, funnel, web form)
**Sequence:**
- Immediate (0–2 min): Auto-SMS to lead + auto-assign rep task (call NOW)
- 2 min: If no contact → second SMS: "Hey {{first_name}}, [Name] here from [Company]. Just saw your request come in — are you dealing with storm damage or looking for a free estimate?"
- 15 min: If no contact → third SMS + email
- 1 hour: If no contact → voicemail drop
- 24 hour: If no contact → follow-up SMS
- 3 days: If no contact → re-engagement SMS
- 7 days: Final nurture SMS

#### Workflow 3: Estimate Appointment Confirmation
**Trigger:** Appointment booked
**Sequence:**
- Immediately: Confirmation SMS + Email (with rep photo, company info, what to expect)

#### Workflow 4: Estimate Follow-Up Sequence
**Trigger:** Estimate completed (stage moved to "Proposal Sent")
**Sequence:**
- Day 1: "Hi {{first_name}}, just checking — did you get a chance to look over the proposal? Any questions I can answer?"

## 6. ROOFING SMS/EMAIL TEMPLATES THAT CONVERT

### SMS Best Practices for Roofing
- **Keep it under 160 characters when possible** (no double-SMS)
- **First name personalization in message 1** always
- **Use urgency around storms, season, or schedule** (works better than generic urgency)
- **Never sound automated** — write like a human, not a bot
- **Ask a question** at the end to prompt a reply (triggers conversation)
- **Response window is 24 hours** — if they don't reply in 24 hours, change the message style, don't repeat

### High-Converting SMS Templates

**Speed-to-Lead (First contact within 60 seconds):**
```
Hey {{first_name}}, this is [Rep Name] at [Company]. Just saw your request come in — are you dealing with storm damage or looking for an estimate on your roof?
```

**Estimate Follow-Up Day 2 (Value-add frame):**
```
{{first_name}} — quick question: do you know when your roof was last inspected? Most homeowners in [City] had hidden storm damage from [recent storm]. Could save you $$$ vs paying out of pocket.
```

**Social Proof SMS:**
```
{{first_name}}, we just finished a full replacement in [Neighborhood] — homeowners paid $0 out of pocket through insurance. Want me to check if you qualify? Takes 20 min.
```

**Urgency/Schedule SMS:**
```
{{first_name}}, we have [2] spots left on our install schedule for [Month]. Want me to hold one? No obligation — just keeps your options open.
```

**Review Request (Highest converting — same day as job complete):**
```
{{first_name}}, the crew wrapped up! How's everything looking? If you're happy with the job, a Google review helps other homeowners find us: [LINK] — takes 60 sec. Thanks so much!
```

**Re-engagement (30-day ghost):**
```
Hey {{first_name}}, checking in from [Company]. It's been a bit — did you end up getting the roof taken care of, or is it still on your list? Happy to revisit if timing's better now.
```

**Storm Alert:**
```
⚠️ [Company]: We're in your area inspecting storm damage. Several roofs on your street qualified for FULL insurance replacement. Free inspection — no pressure. Want one? Reply YES.
```

**Referral Ask:**
```
{{first_name}}, loved working on your roof! Do you know anyone who needs one? We give [amount] for every referral that becomes a job. No catches.
```

### Email Templates

**Estimate Proposal Email:**
- Subject: "Your Roof Estimate from [Company] — [Address]"
- Include: Proposal PDF, rep photo, company reviews link, financing option callout, next steps CTA
- Keep body short — link to proposal, CTA to call or text if questions

**Pre-Appointment Email:**
- Subject: "[Rep Name] is coming Thursday at 2pm — what to expect"
- Include: Rep headshot, brief bio, what happens during inspection, list of documents to have ready (mortgage info, insurance policy), how long it takes
- This email alone reduces no-shows by 15–20%

**Insurance Claim Education Email:**
- Subject: "How roofing insurance claims actually work (and what NOT to say to your adjuster)"
- This is a value-add that positions the contractor as expert and builds trust before the meeting

**Post-Job Thank You Email:**
- Subject: "Your new roof is done! ⭐"
- Include: Before/after photos (uploaded via CompanyCam integration), warranty info, review request, referral program details

## 9. INSURANCE CLAIM WORKFLOW (Deep Dive)

### How Roofing Insurance Claims Work (End-to-End)

**The Typical Insurance Claim Process:**
1. Storm/damage event → Homeowner notices damage
2. Homeowner calls insurance OR contractor contacts homeowner after storm
3. Contractor does FREE inspection → documents damage with photos
4. Homeowner files claim with insurance company (contractor often helps)
5. Insurance assigns an adjuster
6. Adjuster visits property (contractor should be present to advocate)
7. Adjuster provides scope of loss (what's covered)
8. Contractor reviews scope → may supplement if scope is insufficient
9. Insurance sends ACV (Actual Cash Value) check — MINUS depreciation
10. Contractor and homeowner agree on scope + sign contract
11. Work is performed
12. Final inspection
13. Contractor files for depreciation release (RCV)
14. Insurance releases remaining payment (recoverable depreciation)
15. Final payment to contractor

### GHL Insurance Claim Workflow

**Stage 1: Damage Report**
- Intake form (address, damage type, storm date, insurance company)
- Auto-tag: INSURANCE LEAD
- Trigger: Speed-to-lead + "insurance claim specialist" framing

**Stage 2: Free Inspection**
- Book inspection via GHL calendar
- Automated pre-inspection email: "What to have ready" (policy number, etc.)
- Day-of confirmation SMS
- Rep completes inspection → documents with photos (CompanyCam integration)
- Inspection report auto-emailed to homeowner

**Stage 3: Claim Filed**
- SMS: "Great news — based on your inspection, your roof qualifies for insurance replacement. Here's how to file: [Short guide]"
- If homeowner needs help filing: offer to assist (huge value-add)
- Track: Insurance Company name, Claim Number (custom fields in GHL)

**Stage 4: Adjuster Meeting**
- Schedule adjuster meeting via GHL calendar
- Pre-meeting email: "What to say (and not say) to your adjuster"
- Day-before reminder
- After meeting: "How did it go?" check-in

**Stage 5: Approval**
- Capture: Approval amount, scope of loss
- If scope is low: "We identified additional items that should be covered. We can file a supplement on your behalf — no cost to you."
- Contract sent for e-signature via GHL

**Stage 6: Job Completion**
- Standard production workflow (same as retail after this point)
- Post-job: Help homeowner with depreciation release letter to insurance

**Stage 7: Payment**
- Track: ACV check received (yes/no), Supplement filed (yes/no), RCV received (yes/no)
- If supplement filed: follow-up workflow with adjuster name + tracking

### Key Insurance SMS Templates

**After inspection, filing encouragement:**
```
{{first_name}}, based on today's inspection, your roof has [X] areas of qualifying damage. We'll help you through the claim — most homeowners get a full replacement for just their deductible. Want to move forward?
```

**Before adjuster meeting:**
```
Quick reminder: don't mention anything about age, pre-existing conditions, or previous repairs to the adjuster. Just point out the damage we documented. We'll be there with you to make sure everything gets covered.
```

**After low adjuster offer:**
```
The adjuster's initial offer didn't include some items we documented. We're filing a supplement on your behalf — this is very common and we handle it all. You don't need to do anything.
```
