## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The carpet cleaning industry runs on volume, urgency, and trust. The average residential ticket is $150–$350. To build a profitable operation, owners need to stack services (upholstery, tile & grout, area rug, protector), generate recurring revenue from existing clients, and convert one-time jobs into long-term account relationships — especially on the commercial side.

The #1 problem this industry has is **no system**. Most carpet cleaning operators rely on word-of-mouth, manual callbacks, and zero CRM. They win the job, clean the carpet, and forget the customer. Twelve months later, that customer hires someone else because they got a postcard, an ad, or a referral first.

A GHL snapshot built specifically for carpet cleaning solves all of this: automated follow-up, annual cleaning reminders, review generation, bundle upsell sequences, and commercial account management — in one deployable system.

### 1.3 Core Business Pain Points

#### Pain Point #1: Low Average Ticket — Volume Trap
- Average residential job: **$150–$300** (1–3 rooms, hot water extraction)
- Operators need **8–12 jobs/week minimum** just to cover truck payment, chemicals, fuel, and labor
- **No upsell system** = leaving $75–$200 per job on the table (protector, deodorizer, upholstery)
- Fix: Automated bundle upsell workflow triggered immediately after booking confirmation

#### Pain Point #2: No Recurring Revenue Model
- Residential carpet cleaning is recommended every **12–18 months**
- Almost no operators have an automated annual reminder system
- Existing client database = **dormant goldmine** worth tens of thousands of dollars
- Fix: Annual cleaning reminder workflow (12-month trigger from completed job date)

#### Pain Point #3: Review Generation Is Manual / Forgotten
- Carpet cleaning is **review-sensitive** — Google stars drive LSA ranking and click-through rate
- Most operators rely on "hope" to get reviews — they might verbally ask at the end of a job
- Competitors with 200+ reviews dominate LSA over companies with better technicians
- Fix: Post-job review request workflow (SMS + email, 2–4 hours after job completion)

#### Pain Point #4: No Follow-Up on Quotes / Estimates
- Many carpet cleaning calls are "how much for 3 rooms?" — quote given, lead goes cold
- Zero follow-up = revenue walking out the door
- Fix: Instant quote pipeline with automated follow-up sequence (24hr, 72hr, 7-day)

#### Pain Point #5: Commercial Accounts Are Under-Leveraged
- Commercial carpet cleaning contracts = **$300–$3,000+/month recurring**
- Property managers, real estate offices, restaurants, hotels = dream commercial accounts
- Most operators don't have a commercial outreach or proposal system
- Fix: Dedicated commercial pipeline with proposal workflow and account check-in automation

#### Pain Point #6: Booking Friction Kills Conversions
- Carpet cleaning is an **impulse decision** — spilled coffee, company coming, moving out
- If booking requires a phone call, hold time, or email back-and-forth → customer calls the next guy
- Fix: Online instant quote form → calendar booking in one frictionless flow

#### Pain Point #7: No Upsell at Point of Booking
- Most booking flows collect name, address, date — that's it
- No upholstery add-on checkbox. No protector option. No area rug question.
- Average upsell rate goes from ~10% to ~35–45% when presented during the booking flow
- Fix: Multi-step booking form with service add-on selections and dynamic pricing

#### Pain Point #8: Area Rug Drop-Off Is Untracked
- Area rug cleaning (in-facility immersion washing) is **high margin** — often $3–$8/sq ft
- Most operators track it on paper or in their head — no automated pickup/delivery reminders
- Fix: Area rug sub-pipeline with intake, cleaning status, and delivery/pickup notification workflow

#### Pain Point #9: Move-In / Move-Out Is a Missed Revenue Stream
- Real estate agents and property managers need carpet cleaning **every single turnover**
- No operator has a systematic real estate referral program running on autopilot
- Fix: Realtor/property manager referral pipeline + automated check-in sequence

#### Pain Point #10: No Speed-to-Lead on Inbound Calls / Form Fills
- Industry benchmark: **responding within 5 minutes of an inbound lead increases conversion by 9x**
- Most carpet cleaners respond same-day or next-day to form fills
- Fix: Missed call text-back + instant form-fill response workflow

### 4.3 Workflow 3 — Booking Confirmation Sequence

**Trigger:** Appointment booked (calendar event created)

**Sequence:**
1. **Immediate:** SMS — "You're booked! 🎉 [BusinessName] will be at [Address] on [Date] between [TimeWindow]. We use truck-mount hot water extraction — the gold standard for deep cleaning. Questions? Reply here."
2. **Immediate:** Email — Full booking confirmation with: date/time, address, services booked, prep instructions (move small furniture, vacuum beforehand if possible, clear path to water source), technician name if known, cancellation policy.
3. **24 hours before:** SMS — "Reminder: Your carpet cleaning is tomorrow, [Date] between [TimeWindow]. Our tech will call/text 30 min before arrival. Reply CONFIRM to confirm or RESCHEDULE to change. 🧹"
4. **2 hours before:** SMS — "[TechName] is on his way! ETA: approximately [Time]. He'll be arriving soon to handle your [services] with our truck-mount system."

**Prep Instructions to Include in Confirmation Email:**
- Vacuum lightly before arrival (we'll do a deeper pre-vacuum but it helps)
- Move small/fragile items off carpet
- Ensure a parking spot within 100ft of your front door (truck-mount hose reach)
- Identify the closest water source (garden hose hookup or utility sink)
- Keep pets in a separate room during service
- Drying time: 4–8 hours depending on humidity and airflow; open windows if possible

## SECTION 6: BOOKING FLOW ARCHITECTURE

### 6.1 Online Instant Quote Form (Multi-Step)

The booking form is the conversion centerpiece. It must be:
- **Fast** (3–5 steps max on mobile)
- **Smart** (conditional logic to show relevant add-ons)
- **Low-friction** (no account creation, no phone call required)
- **Qualifying** (collect enough info to price accurately)

**Step 1 — What Are You Looking to Clean?**
- [ ] Carpet / Area Rugs
- [ ] Upholstery (sofas, chairs, mattress)
- [ ] Tile & Grout
- [ ] All of the above / Not sure

**Step 2 — Tell Us About Your Space:**
- *If carpet selected:* Number of rooms (1, 2, 3, 4, 5+), any hallways/stairs?

## APPENDIX A: INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Hot Water Extraction (HWE) | The industry-standard carpet cleaning method; pressurized hot water + solution injected into carpet and extracted immediately. Also called "steam cleaning" (misnomer). |
| Truck-Mount | A carpet cleaning unit mounted in a van or truck; more powerful heat and suction than portable units. Premium positioning. |
| Portable Unit | A self-contained carpet cleaning machine; used in high-rises, condos, or where truck-mount hose can't reach. |
| Pre-Spray / Pre-Treatment | Chemical solution applied to carpet before hot water extraction; allowed to dwell and break down soils before main cleaning. |
| Dwell Time | The time a pre-spray or encapsulation solution sits on the carpet before being agitated or extracted. Critical for effectiveness. |
| Encapsulation | Low-moisture cleaning method; detergent crystallizes around soil particles which are then vacuumed up. Used commercially for faster dry time. |
| Scotchgard / Protector | Fluorochemical carpet protector applied after cleaning; repels liquids and soil, makes vacuuming more effective. Also called "fabric protector" or "fluorochemical protector." |
| Enzyme Deodorizer | Biological treatment that neutralizes pet urine and organic odors at the molecular level. Not a masking spray. |
| Pet Deodorizer Treatment | Broader category including enzyme treatments, UV light inspection, sub-surface extraction for urine below the carpet backing. |
| Area Rug | Loose-lay rugs (not wall-to-wall carpet). Cleaned in-plant with immersion washing, dusting, and hand/machine washing. |
| Immersion Washing | Full submersion cleaning for area rugs in a controlled plant environment. |
| Upholstery Cleaning | Extraction cleaning of fabric-covered furniture: sofas, loveseats, chairs, ottomans, mattresses. |
| Tile & Grout Cleaning | High-pressure steam cleaning of tile surfaces + grout lines. Often includes grout sealing as an upsell. |
| Water Extraction | Emergency removal of standing water from carpet, pad, and subfloor after flooding, pipe burst, or appliance leak. |
| Truck-Mount vs Portable | Key positioning tool: truck-mount is preferred for most residential; portable is the solution (not compromise) for condos and high-rises. |
| CRI (Carpet and Rug Institute) | The industry authority for carpet care standards and cleaning method certification. CRI-certified methods meet warranty-compliance standards. |
| Move-In / Move-Out Clean | Carpet cleaning between tenants or after a home sale. High-volume, time-sensitive, often involves full-property service. |
