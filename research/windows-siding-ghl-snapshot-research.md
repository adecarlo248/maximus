## 1. INDUSTRY OVERVIEW & MARKET CONTEXT

### What This Industry Is
Windows and siding is a **high-ticket, considered-purchase** home improvement vertical. Average job values:
- **Window replacement:** $800–$2,500/window installed. Full-house replacement: $8,000–$35,000+
- **Vinyl siding:** $6,000–$25,000 depending on sq footage and style (lap siding, board and batten, shake)
- **James Hardie / fibre cement:** $15,000–$40,000+ — premium tier
- **Storm damage claim replacement:** Often insurance-funded, full exterior scope $20,000–$80,000+

### Product Vocabulary (Use In All Content)

**Window Types:**
- Double-hung, single-hung, casement, awning, sliding, picture, bay window, bow window, garden window, hopper, egress window
- Skylight, roof window, sun tunnel

**Window Features & Specs:**
- Low-E coating (Low-Emissivity glass)
- Triple-pane, double-pane, argon gas fill, krypton gas fill
- U-factor (lower = better insulation; ≤0.25 for ENERGY STAR Canada)
- Solar Heat Gain Coefficient (SHGC) — lower in south/west facing, higher in north
- Air Leakage (AL) rating — lower is better
- ER (Energy Rating) — Canada-specific composite score; ≥25 for ENERGY STAR
- Visible Transmittance (VT)
- Warm-edge spacer, foam-filled frame, thermal break
- Grid patterns: colonial, prairie, farmhouse, SDL (simulated divided light)
- Fibrex, uPVC, vinyl, aluminum-clad wood, fibreglass (Pella Impervia, Marvin Integrity)
- NFRC label (National Fenestration Rating Council — USA) / CSA A440 (Canada)

**Siding Types & Materials:**
- Vinyl siding: lap/horizontal, vertical/board and batten, Dutch lap, beaded, scallop/shake
- James Hardie (fibre cement): HardiePlank, HardiePanel, HardieShingle, HardieSoffit
- Engineered wood: LP SmartSide
- Aluminum siding (legacy)
- Stucco, EIFS (Exterior Insulation Finishing System)
- Cedar shake (real wood — premium)
- Metal cladding (Longboard, steel)

**Exterior Trim & Related:**
- Soffit — underside of roof overhang
- Fascia — board at roofline edge
- Frieze board — trim between top of siding and soffit
- J-channel, corner post, starter strip, drip cap
- House wrap / vapour barrier
- Rigid foam insulation board (outboard insulation)
- Window wrapping / capping (aluminum wrap around frames)
- Caulking, flashing, weep screed

**Storm Damage Terms:**
- Hail impact, hail spatter/bruising, dents, cracking
- Wind-driven rain infiltration
- Delamination (vinyl/composite layers separating)
- Insurance adjuster, public adjuster, supplement
- Scope of loss, line items, Xactimate pricing
- RCV (Replacement Cost Value) vs. ACV (Actual Cash Value)
- Depreciation hold-back, recoverable depreciation
- Certificate of Completion (for mortgage company releases)

### Canadian Specific Context (Ontario Focus)
- **ENERGY STAR** certification administered by NRCan (Natural Resources Canada)
- **Canada Greener Homes Grant** (federal — up to $5,600 for windows/doors — program in wind-down/replacement cycles; always check current status)
- **Enbridge Home Efficiency Rebate Plus (HER+):** Ontario gas customers; up to $5,000 for insulation/windows
- **OPA / IESO programs** — Ontario utility rebate programs
- **Municipal rebates** — some Ontario municipalities (Toronto, Ottawa) have additional top-ups
- **HST:** 13% in Ontario — financing deals often pitched as "own it for just $X/month + HST"
- **TARION warranty** (new build) vs. **contractor warranty** (renovation)
- **Building permit** — often required for structural changes, egress windows, anything changing rough opening size
- **BILD / OHBA** — industry associations; membership is a trust signal
- **ESA (Electrical Safety Authority)** — relevant if window wells involve lighting/outlets

## 2. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS

Understanding these is critical for writing copy, building workflows, and positioning the snapshot to owners.

### 2.1 Speed-to-Lead Problem
Window and siding leads are **high intent but short window**. Homeowner searches Google at 9pm, fills a form, and calls three other companies by 9am. The company that follows up first with a phone call or text wins the appointment 78% of the time (industry data).

**Pain:** Most window/siding companies have ZERO automated follow-up. Owner gets the email notification while driving, calls back 4 hours later, and the prospect already booked with Window World.

**GHL Solution:** Immediate SMS + AI voice or rep call trigger within 60 seconds of form submission.

### 2.2 Multi-Quote Shoppers
Homeowners routinely get 3–5 quotes on a $20K+ purchase. This is a **trust and urgency problem**, not a price problem. Companies with a better follow-up sequence and educational content convert higher even at the same price.

**Pain:** No nurture sequence. One call, no answer, lead goes cold.

**GHL Solution:** 14-day automated nurture with education content (why triple-pane, what ENERGY STAR means, how rebates work) — builds trust before the in-home meeting.

### 2.3 Insurance Storm Damage Complexity
Hail or wind events generate massive lead volume — but the process is completely different from retail. Homeowners don't understand their policy, fear the deductible, and need hand-holding through the claims process.

**Pain:** Sales reps don't know how to nurture a homeowner through a 30–90 day insurance claim. They do the inspection, submit photos, then never follow up consistently.

**GHL Solution:** Dedicated insurance claim pipeline with milestone-based workflows: "Claim Filed," "Adjuster Scheduled," "Approved," "Supplement Pending," "Ready to Schedule."

### 2.4 Seasonal Demand Spikes & Drops
Windows and siding are a **shoulder season business** in Canada. Peak: April–June, September–October. Dead: December–February.

**Pain:** No off-season lead nurture. Companies go quiet all winter and scramble in spring.

**GHL Solution:** Off-season rebate campaign, pre-booking workflow, "lock in spring pricing" campaign triggered in January–February.

### 2.5 Financing Complexity
Many homeowners can't write a $25,000 check. Financing is the #1 conversion lever in this vertical — but most companies just say "we offer financing" with no follow-up.

**Pain:** Prospect asked about financing in the estimate follow-up, rep said "I'll send info," never did.

**GHL Solution:** Financing awareness workflow — automated email/SMS series explaining payment options, monthly payment calculator, call-to-action to book a "financing conversation."

### 2.6 Measurement Accuracy & Project Scope
Windows are measured to the 1/8 inch. Siding jobs require accurate square footage, rake measurements, and trim lineal footage. Mistakes cost money.

**Pain:** No pre-consultation intake form. Rep shows up to the home without knowing what the client wants, how many windows, current product type, or whether permits are needed.

**GHL Solution:** Pre-consultation intake form sent 24 hours before the in-home appointment. Collects: number/type of windows, photos of existing product, HOA restrictions, permit history, preferred styles.

### 2.7 Permit Requirements & Municipal Complexity
Ontario building permits are required for:
- Structural changes (enlarging rough opening)
- Egress windows in basements
- Adding skylights
- Changing cladding type in some heritage districts

**Pain:** Sales rep promises a 3-week timeline, permit takes 6 weeks, customer is furious.

**GHL Solution:** Permit status pipeline stage + automated "permit update" touchpoint to keep client informed.

### 2.8 Long Sales Cycle on High-Ticket Jobs
Full-house fibre cement reclad at $45,000 is not a same-day decision. Sales cycle can be 30–90 days.

**Pain:** No long-term nurture. Rep quoted the job, followed up once, and assumed they lost it.

**GHL Solution:** 90-day premium lead nurture sequence — case studies, project photos, testimonials, financing education, seasonal urgency.

### 2.9 Referral & Neighbour Opportunity Missed
Every installed job is a billboard on the street. Every neighbour who walks by is a warm lead.

**Pain:** Company installs beautiful new windows, does nothing to capitalize on the 15 neighbours who drove by and noticed.

**GHL Solution:** Post-install neighbour campaign — door hanger + automated follow-up to any neighbour who calls or texts in.

### 2.10 Review Generation Gap
Google reviews are the #1 trust signal for local home improvement companies. Most companies do nothing systematic.

**Pain:** Happy customer, zero follow-up for review. Angry customer, zero reputation management.

**GHL Solution:** Automated post-install review request sequence at Day 3, Day 7, Day 21 after completion.

## 3. COMPETITIVE SNAPSHOT LANDSCAPE

### 3.1 What Currently Exists on Market

#### Generic "Home Services" Snapshots
Most available snapshots (from HL Marketplace, Extendly, SaaS accelerators) are **generic home services** templates covering roofing, HVAC, plumbing. These typically include:
- 1 generic pipeline
- 3–5 simple automations
- No industry-specific language
- No insurance storm damage track
- No rebate campaigns
- No neighbour campaign

#### Roofing-Specific Snapshots (Most Developed Adjacent)
Roofing snapshots are the most mature in the home exterior vertical. Examples from the market include:
- **Extendly Roofing Snapshot** — strong insurance claim workflow, decent speed-to-lead, basic review system
- **Lone Star Roofing Snapshot** — heavy on storm chaser/insurance track; limited retail
- Various YouTube creators (Dom Bavaro, Jake Zober, SaaS-preneur channels) — publish walkthroughs but not windows-specific

**What roofing snapshots have that windows snapshots DON'T:**
- Insurance claim pipeline stages
- Xactimate education nurture
- Public adjuster referral workflow
- Storm event rapid-deploy campaign

#### Windows/Siding-Specific Gaps
After thorough research, there is **no dominant, purpose-built windows & siding snapshot** on the market. The vertical is underserved. This is a significant opportunity for 1app to own this niche with a specialized, industry-language-rich build.

**What doesn't exist yet (our differentiators):**
- ENERGY STAR / government rebate-focused campaign sequences
- Canadian-specific content (HST, Greener Homes, Enbridge HER+, NRCan)
- Triple-track pipeline (retail / insurance storm / commercial)
- Product education sequence (triple-pane vs. double-pane, U-factor explained, vinyl vs. Hardie)
- Permit status stage in pipeline
- Pre-consultation measurement intake form
- Seasonal demand campaign triggers
- Neighbour campaign with door hanger + digital follow-up
- Financing awareness drip
- "Estimate On Hold" re-engagement for long-cycle leads

### 3.2 What the Market Is Missing
The white space in the market is a **comprehensive, industry-specific windows & siding snapshot** that:
1. Speaks the language (U-factor, double-hung, Hardie board, soffit, ACV)
2. Handles three completely different buyer journeys (retail, insurance, commercial)
3. Has a Canadian rebate campaign built in
4. Automates the neighbour and referral flywheel
5. Includes a financing nurture track
6. Has a pre-consultation intake form that makes reps more effective

## 6. LEAD SOURCE STRATEGY

### 6.1 Google Local Services Ads (LSA)
**Best for:** High-intent, "near me" searches  
**Cost:** Pay-per-lead ($30–$120/lead depending on market)  
**Lead quality:** Very high — homeowner actively searching  
**GHL integration:** Direct lead form → GHL contact via Zapier or native LSA integration  
**Key tags:** `source_lsa`, `google_verified`  
**Workflow:** Speed-to-lead CRITICAL — LSA leads go cold in 30 minutes

### 6.2 Facebook / Instagram Ads
**Best for:** Storm damage campaigns, rebate campaigns, seasonal pushes  
**Creative:** Before/after transformations, rebate education videos, "check if you qualify" quiz  
**Audience targeting:**
- Homeowners 35–65
- Household income $75K+
- Home value filter ($300K+)
- Custom audience: past customers for referral ads
- Lookalike: 2–3% lookalike from past customers
**Lead form:** Facebook native lead form (lower friction) → GHL via Zapier  
**GHL tags:** `source_facebook`, `source_instagram`

### 6.3 Storm Chaser / Event-Based Canvassing
**Context:** After a significant hail event (1" diameter hail = window/siding damage threshold)  
**Method:** Door-to-door canvassing in affected postal codes; free inspection offer  
**Tools:** Hail tracking apps (Hail Trace, Storm Report) to identify affected areas  
**Lead entry:** Rep enters contact in GHL mobile app in driveway  
**GHL tags:** `source_storm_canvass`, `insurance_track`, storm event date  
**Workflow:** Insurance Storm Damage pipeline auto-triggers

### 6.4 Insurance Agent / Public Adjuster Referrals
**Relationship:** Insurance agents refer clients with storm damage for inspection  
**Public adjusters:** Often work alongside contractors to maximize claim value  
**GHL:** Referral source tracking custom field; automated "thank you" to referral partner  
**Key:** Set up a referral partner sub-pipeline or contact tag for tracking referral ROI

### 6.5 Door Knocking (Canvassing)
**Method:** Systematic neighborhood canvassing — especially in older neighborhoods (1980s–2000s homes, original windows, original siding)  
**Timing:** Spring and fall, after storms  
**Entry:** Mobile GHL app + custom canvassing form (address, homeowner name, interest level, estimated window count)  
**Tag:** `source_door_knock`

### 6.6 Referral / Word of Mouth
**Most valuable lead type** — closes at 3–5x the rate of cold leads  
**GHL:** Referral source field on all contacts; referral request workflow post-install  
**Incentive:** Consider referral reward program (gift card, discount on next service)  
**Tag:** `source_referral`, referral source contact linked

### 6.7 Yard Sign / Job Site Signage
**Simple:** Install sign during every job with company phone number  
**Track:** Unique tracking number or "mention yard sign" for source attribution

## 9. ENERGY STAR & GOVERNMENT REBATE CAMPAIGN

### 9.1 Canadian ENERGY STAR Window Requirements
For ENERGY STAR certification in Canada (NRCan), windows must meet zone-specific criteria:

| Climate Zone | U-factor (max) | ER (min) |
|---|---|---|
| Zone 1 (South BC, southern coastal) | 1.40 | 25 |
| Zone 2 (Most of Ontario, Quebec, BC interior) | 1.20 | 29 |
| Zone 3 (Northern Ontario, Prairies) | 1.08 | 34 |

*Note: values are in W/(m²·K) for Canadian format — convert from US U-factors (W/m²K = BTU/hr·ft²·°F × 5.678)*

**For Ontario (Zone 2):**
- U-factor ≤ 1.20 W/(m²·K) [≈ 0.21 in US units]
- ER ≥ 29
- Most quality triple-pane vinyl windows from brands like Jeld-Wen, Loewen, Gentek, Ideal Window, Windows by Portes Patio Rolly, or Strassburger will meet this threshold

### 9.2 Enbridge HER+ (Home Efficiency Rebate Plus)
- **Who qualifies:** Ontario customers with an Enbridge Gas (Union Gas) account
- **Window rebate:** Up to $250 per window (current program — verify current amounts)
- **Requirements:** Pre and post energy assessment by a NRCan-registered energy advisor
- **Contractor eligibility:** Contractor does not need to be registered; homeowner applies
- **Process:** Energy audit → upgrade → post-audit → rebate paid to homeowner
- **Approximate rebate timeline:** 8–16 weeks after job completion
- **GHL implication:** Build "Rebate Application in Progress" tag and touchpoint sequence

### 9.3 Canada Greener Homes Grant / Loan
- **Grant:** $125–$5,600 for eligible upgrades (windows/doors eligible)
- **Loan:** Up to $40,000 at 0% interest for eligible homeowners
- **Status note:** The grant component was paused/replaced with the loan in 2024; always verify current program status with NRCan
- **Process:** Requires pre and post energy audit by registered advisor
- **GHL:** Rebate education sequence; link to official Canada.ca portal

- "Have us on the phone during the adjuster visit" offer

**Post-Adjuster Check-In Call:**
- Rep calls within 24 hours of adjuster visit to review scope
- If scope is low: explain supplement process; offer to write supplement on client's behalf
- If scope is adequate: move to contract stage

**Supplement Workflow:**
- Rep files supplement with Xactimate line items for missed items
- GHL stage: "Supplement Pending"
- Automated weekly check-in to homeowner
- On supplement approval: move to contract stage

**Certificate of Completion:**
- Generated from GHL proposal/document tool or external (PandaDoc)
- Sent to homeowner AND mortgage company (if applicable)
- Tag applied: "Certificate Issued" — triggers recoverable depreciation follow-up

## 12. REVIEW GENERATION SYSTEM

### 12.1 Timing Strategy
Research consistently shows review requests convert best at:
- **Day 3** after job completion — excitement is fresh, job is still top of mind
- **Day 7** — second touch for those who didn't act on day 3
- **Day 14** — final automated attempt; personal call from rep at day 21

### 12.2 Review Request Best Practices
- **Direct link:** Always use direct Google review link (no extra clicks)
- **Personalize:** Reference the specific job — "love how the new casements look on your front elevation"
- **Mobile-first:** Most reviews submitted on mobile — short, simple message with one link
- **Negative interception:** Offer a direct feedback channel BEFORE the review request — "If anything wasn't perfect, tell us here first"

### 12.3 Reputation Management
- **5-star:** Thank within 24 hours; screenshot and post to social media (with permission)
- **4-star:** Thank and ask what could have been 5-star — use for internal improvement
- **3-star or below:** Personal call from owner/manager within 24 hours — resolution protocol
- **1-star review published:** Public response template (professional, empathetic, invite offline resolution)

### 12.4 Multi-Platform Review Strategy
**Primary:** Google (most impact for local SEO)  
**Secondary:** HomeStars (critical in Canada), Houzz, Facebook, Yelp, Better Business Bureau  
**HomeStars specific:** Canada's #1 home improvement review platform. Getting "Best of HomeStars" award is a significant trust signal for Canadian homeowners.

### 12.5 GHL Assets for Review System
- [ ] Review request SMS template (ST-41, ST-42)

## 13. SNAPSHOT DIFFERENTIATORS

### 13.1 What Makes the 1app Windows & Siding Snapshot Stand Out

**1. Industry Language Baked In**
Every template, form, and workflow uses authentic trade vocabulary — double-hung, casement, U-factor, ER rating, argon fill, HardiePlank, soffit, fascia, Xactimate, ACV, RCV. This instantly signals credibility to the window/siding company owner, unlike generic "home services" snapshots.

**2. Three Distinct Pipelines**
Most snapshots have one generic pipeline. We have three:
- Retail quote (highest volume)
- Insurance storm damage (highest ticket)
- Commercial (lowest volume, highest value)

Each has its own stage logic, automations, and touchpoints — no mixing of buyer journeys.

**3. Canadian Rebate Campaign Built In**
ENERGY STAR, Enbridge HER+, Canada Greener Homes — this is a significant competitive advantage in Canadian markets. No other GHL snapshot we've found addresses this with actual content and workflows.

**4. Pre-Consultation Intake Form**
The intake form sent before the in-home appointment makes every rep 3× more effective. They arrive knowing window count, existing material, problems, style preferences, and HOA status. This is not in any other window/siding snapshot.

**5. Storm Event Rapid-Deploy Campaign**
A dedicated storm response workflow with SMS blast to existing customers, ad creative swap logic, and canvassing team coordination. Modeled on what works in roofing — adapted specifically for windows and siding damage profiles.

**6. Neighbour Campaign Automation**
Post-install geo-targeted digital ads + door hanger task trigger + unique tracking phone number and URL. Complete neighbourhood flywheel baked into the post-install workflow.

**7. Financing Awareness Drip**
A dedicated financing track with monthly payment estimates, application CTAs, and follow-up sequence. Financial barrier removal is the #1 conversion accelerator in this vertical.

**8. Permit Status Pipeline Stage**
Often overlooked — permits cause delays that kill client satisfaction. Building permit tracking into the pipeline and sending homeowners proactive updates keeps them calm and reduces churn.

**9. Product Education Sequences**
3 educational emails built in — triple-pane vs. double-pane, U-factor explained, vinyl vs. Hardie board. These build trust and authority between the quote and the signed contract.

**10. Seasonal Campaign Logic**
Winter pre-book campaign (January–March), spring push, storm season rapid-deploy. Seasonal triggers built into the system to reduce owner's marketing burden.
