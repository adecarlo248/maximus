Google Ads Note

For sponsored HVAC placement, the two best options are usually Google Local Services Ads and Google Search Ads. The recommendation is to start with the foundation first so paid traffic is not sent into a leaky system.

No honest provider can guarantee exact Google ranking, lead volume, or #1 placement. What can be controlled is profile quality, reviews, website conversion, follow-up speed, tracking, local SEO foundation, and ad setup.
