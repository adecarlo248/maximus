Purpose: One-page guide for sales associates and contracts teams explaining what each Tier sells, who it's for, key benefits, pricing (finalized), onboarding checklist, contract essentials, upsell path, and quick scripts.

OVERVIEW (pricing [certain])
- Tier 1 — "Never Miss a Lead" — $197/mo
- Tier 2 — "Always Open" — $397/mo
- Tier 3 — "Full Autopilot" — $597/mo

TIER 1 — Never Miss a Lead ($197/mo)
- Elevator: Stop losing leads from missed calls and slow follow-up.
- What we deliver:
  - Missed Call Text Back — automated 3-step SMS follow-up that re-engages missed callers
  - Booking calendar + booking widget (connect & basic calendar setup)
  - Confirmation + reminder sequence (24hr and 1hr SMS/email reminders)
  - Basic reporting: bookings + SMS activity
- Ideal client: Small local biz with high missed-call volume (trades, service pros)
- Key benefit to sell: More booked appointments from leads you already pay to get.
- Common objections & replies:
  - "We already have someone answering": "This works 24/7 on missed calls and boosts bookings without adding headcount." 
  - "Not sure of ROI": "Typical clients see a measurable bump in booked estimates within 30 days — we only charge $197/mo to capture that missed revenue."
- Onboarding checklist (sales -> ops handoff):
  1. Confirm phone number & calendar availability
  2. Add client to sub-account and assign phone number
  3. Configure 3-step SMS sequence and booking widget
  4. Test missed-call flow and booking confirmation
  5. Activate reporting and confirm first-week check-in

TIER 2 — Always Open ($397/mo)
- Elevator: All of Tier 1, plus a 24/7 AI voice receptionist that answers, qualifies, and books appointments.
- What we deliver (everything in Tier 1 plus):
  - AI Voice Agent (24/7) — answers calls, asks qualifying questions, books calendar slots
  - Voice → calendar integration and lead handoff
  - Improved call capture & qualification
- Ideal client: Businesses that want live-call coverage without hiring staff (higher call volume, value-per-lead > $40)
- Key benefit to sell: More qualified appointments and fewer missed revenue opportunities — works like hiring a 24/7 front desk at a fraction of the cost.
- Upsell triggers from Tier 1: client mentions inbound calls outside business hours or staff burnout handling calls.
- Onboarding checklist (adds to Tier 1):
  1. Script & qualification questions with client
  2. Voice agent persona selection + brief voice test
  3. Map booking rules, service types, and call routing
  4. Test live call flow with 3–5 calls during setup week

TIER 3 — Full Autopilot ($597/mo)
- Elevator: Everything in Tier 2 plus review automation, nurture sequences, and monthly performance reporting.
- What we deliver (everything in Tier 2 plus):
  - Review request automation (reputation & Google reviews flow)
  - Lead nurture sequences (48h follow-up, longer nurture for no-shows)
  - Monthly performance report with recommendations
  - Priority support (faster turnarounds for configuration changes)
- Ideal client: Businesses ready to convert repeat customers and scale lead conversion with automated reputation & nurture systems
- Key benefit to sell: Automates the full lead → appointment → review lifecycle so growth compounds without extra staff.
- Upsell path: Add social planner, website build, or ad management as separate packages.

CONTRACT BASICS (recommended default — please confirm)
- Billing: Monthly subscription billed in advance (auto-renew) — price per above.
- Minimum term: Month-to-month with 30-day cancellation (recommended; we can offer annual discount on request).
- Setup: One-time onboarding included in first month (complex setups may carry a quoted setup fee).
- SLA / turnaround: Standard config within 3–7 business days; voice agent rollout and testing within 7–14 days depending on complexity.
- Deliverables: Confirmed list of connected phone number, booking calendar, SMS sequences, voice agent script (if Tier 2+), and access to reporting.
- Payment methods: Card on file; invoices on request.
- Data & privacy: Client retains ownership of leads; we store call logs and SMS metadata for service delivery. (Add a privacy/data clause in formal contract.)

SIMPLE CONTRACT CLAUSES TO INCLUDE (ops / legal can formalize)
- Service description: Summarize selected Tier and included features
- Billing: monthly price, billing date, auto-renew, late fees
- Term & termination: month-to-month, 30-day notice
- Setup & scope changes: scope changes may require additional fees and a timeline
- Support: standard vs priority (Tier 3 gets priority)
- Liability & limitations: typical SaaS liability cap and data handling note

SELLING & ONBOARDING FLOW (for sales associates)
1. Qualify call: Ask about missed call volume, average job value, current booking process, and priority hours.
2. Recommend Tier: If high missed calls and no answer tech → Tier 1; if high inbound need or value-per-lead justifies automation → Tier 2; if they want reviews & automation → Tier 3.
3. Pitch hooks: "You stop losing money from missed calls", "This books more appointments without hiring", "We increase qualified leads and automate reviews".
4. Close & contract: Send simple scope summary + signup link; collect card info and desired go-live date.
5. Handoff: Fill the onboarding checklist and create a support ticket with the ops team.

QUICK SCRIPT (30–60s)
- "We fix missed revenue: for $197/month we automate missed-call follow-up and book appointments for you. If you want 24/7 call answering that qualifies and books for you, the next step up is $397/month. The full package at $597/month adds review requests and automated nurture so bookings and reputation grow without you needing extra staff. Which one sounds like it fits the problem you're trying to solve?"

FAQ (sales-friendly)
- Q: How fast do we see bookings? A: Many clients see increased booked appointments in 2–4 weeks after activation.
- Q: Is there a long-term contract? A: Default is month-to-month with 30-day cancellation — we can offer annual discounts if they want to lock in savings.
- Q: Do we build websites? A: Website builds are a separate one-time service — mention as an upsell.
