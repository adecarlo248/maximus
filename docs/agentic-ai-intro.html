What Is Agentic AI?

And how it can save you hours every week — even from your phone

Regular AI vs. Agentic AI

Regular AI (like using ChatGPT as Google) — you ask a question, it answers. You still do all the work.

Agentic AI — you give it a task, it thinks through the steps, uses tools, makes decisions, and gets it done. You step away.

"Regular AI is a smart employee who only works when you're talking to them. Agentic AI is a manager — you say 'handle this,' and they figure it out and report back."

Start Today — From Your Phone (Free)

Download ChatGPT or Claude — both free on iOS and Android. Then shift how you use them:

❌ Don't do this (using it like Google)
"What's a good price for painting a room?"
✅ Do this instead (giving it a job)
"I'm a painter. A customer wants their 12x14 living room painted, two coats. Write me a professional quote email with my pricing at $X."

The difference: you go from getting information to getting work done.
