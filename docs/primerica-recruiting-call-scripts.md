# Primerica Recruiting Call Scripts — Tony DeCarlo

## 1) Warm Market Quick Call

**Goal:** Book a short look at the business opportunity / BPM. Do not explain everything on the phone.

### Opener
“Hey [Name], it’s Tony. Quick one — I’m expanding my financial services business with Primerica and I thought of you because you’re sharp, good with people, and I think you’d at least understand the opportunity.

I’m not calling to pitch you over the phone. I just wanted to see if you’d be open to taking a quick look at what we’re building and seeing if it makes sense for you or someone you know.”

### If Interested
“Perfect. The easiest next step is a short overview. It’s usually 20–30 minutes, no pressure. If it makes sense, we talk next steps. If not, no big deal.

Are you better during the day or evening?”

### If They Ask “What is it?”
“It’s a part-time business in financial services — we help families with protection, debt, savings, and financial education. The business side is helping expand the team and train people who want to learn the industry.

Rather than butcher it in 60 seconds, I’d rather show you the overview properly. Are you open to looking?”

### Close for Appointment
“Cool. I’ve got [time option 1] or [time option 2]. Which works better?”

## 2) No Sponsor / Previously Licensed Agent Call

**Goal:** Reconnect with people who were licensed or listed publicly, identify if they’re still open to financial services, book a deeper conversation.

### Opener
“Hi [Name], this is Tony DeCarlo. I’ll be quick — I came across your name connected to the insurance/financial services industry here in Ontario and it looked like you may not currently be attached to a sponsor.

I’m with Primerica and we’re expanding. I wanted to see if you’re still open to being active in the industry, either part-time or full-time.”

### If They Say “Maybe / What’s this about?”
“Totally fair. I’m not trying to hard-sell you. I’m looking for people who already have some familiarity with the industry and may want a cleaner path to get active again, build a client base, or plug into a team.

The next step would just be a short conversation to see where you’re at and whether there’s a fit.”

### Close
“Are you still interested in the financial services space at all, or have you moved on completely?”

If yes:
“Good. Then let’s do this properly — 15–20 minutes, I’ll ask where you’re at, show you what we’re building, and we’ll see if there’s a fit. Are you better today or tomorrow?”

## 5) Objection Handles

### “How did you get my number?”
“Fair question. I found your name publicly connected to the insurance/financial services industry and did some basic contact research from there. If now’s not a good time, no problem — I was just reaching out to see if you’re still open to the industry.”

### “I’m not interested.”
“No worries. Just so I don’t bug you again — are you completely out of financial services now, or just not looking at anything new right now?”

### “What’s Primerica?”
“Primerica is a financial services company. We focus on helping middle-income families with financial education, protection, debt strategies, and building a business through recruiting and training licensed reps. The best way to understand it is a short overview — not a rushed phone explanation.”

### “Is this MLM?”
“I get why you’d ask. Primerica is licensed financial services — people have to be properly licensed to do the business. There is a team-building side, yes, but the foundation is helping families and training people properly. I’m not looking for hype people — I’m looking for people who want to learn the business the right way.”

### “I don’t have time.”
“That’s exactly why I usually start with a short look. Most people who do this start part-time. The question isn’t ‘do you have tons of time?’ — it’s whether it’s worth learning enough to decide. Could you spare 20 minutes to look?”

### “Send me info.”
“I can, but honestly info without context usually just sits there. Let me send you a quick overview link/message, and if it catches your attention we’ll book 15 minutes. Fair?”

### “What does it pay?”
“It’s commission-based, so it depends on licensing, activity, and production — I don’t want to throw out numbers like a gimmick. What I can show you is how the business model works, how reps are trained, and what realistic next steps look like.”

---

## 6) Call Discipline

- Don’t explain everything. Book the look.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Keep tone calm, curious, and confident.
- Track every call: no answer, not interested, maybe later, booked, follow-up.
- Best close: “Are you open to taking a look?”
