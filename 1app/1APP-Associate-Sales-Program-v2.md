# 1APP Associate Sales Program — v2

**Purpose:** Create a clean, controlled structure for people who want to sell 1APP without turning the business into chaos.

**Core rule:** Associates help bring in qualified clients. 1APP owns the brand, pricing, client accounts, fulfilment standards, and final approval of every deal.

---

## 1. Program Positioning

1APP Associates are independent sales/referral partners who introduce local businesses to 1APP and help book qualified audits/demos.

They are **not employees**, **not owners**, and **not authorized to make promises outside approved 1APP offers**.

**Plain-English pitch for associates:**

> 1APP helps small businesses stop losing leads, automate follow-up, book more jobs, and save time using one simple business app. Your job is to start conversations with business owners, book qualified audits, and help them see where they're leaking time or money.

---

## 2. Best-Fit Associate Profile

Good associates should already be comfortable talking to business owners.

**Ideal people:**
- Connected locally
- Comfortable with phone, DM, text, and in-person conversations
- Coachable
- Not afraid of follow-up
- Understand small business pain
- Can represent the brand professionally
- Will use the approved pitch instead of making random claims

**Avoid:**
- People who only want passive referral money
- People who exaggerate income claims
- People who discount without permission
- People who cannot follow a simple CRM process
- Anyone who might damage the 1APP brand for a quick commission

## 4. Associate Levels

### Level 1 — Referral Associate

**Best for:** People who can introduce warm business contacts but do not want to run full sales calls.

**Responsibilities:**
- Identify potential business clients
- Make warm introductions
- Book the business owner into a 1APP audit/demo
- Submit lead details into the approved form/CRM

**Can do:**
- Share approved 1APP materials
- Invite business owners to a free audit
- Make introductions to Tony/Justin/1APP sales team

**Cannot do:**
- Quote custom pricing
- Promise features, timelines, guarantees, or results
- Run independent demos unless approved
- Claim to be an owner/partner of 1APP

**Compensation:**
- Flat referral bonus per plan (see table above)
- No recurring commission at this level
- Paid only on collected revenue after any refund window/chargeback risk clears

---

### Level 2 — Sales Associate

**Best for:** People who can prospect, qualify, follow up, and help close using the approved 1APP sales process.

**Responsibilities:**
- Prospect businesses in approved niches
- Book audits/demos
- Complete basic discovery using the 1APP qualification checklist
- Track all activity in CRM
- Follow up until closed/lost
- Bring Tony/Justin into the close when needed

**Can do:**
- Prospect cold/warm leads
- Use approved scripts and offers
- Present standard packages
- Help close standard Starter/Growth/Revenue Engine packages

**Cannot do without approval:**
- Discount pricing
- Offer custom builds
- Modify contract terms
- Promise same-day launches
- Sell outside approved niches/offers

**Compensation:**

| What | Rate |
|------|------|
| Setup fee commission | **15%** of setup fee collected |
| Monthly recurring | **20%** of MRR — capped at 12 paid months per client |

The 12-month cap exists at this level. After 12 months, recurring commission on that client stops. This is the key structural difference between Level 2 and Level 3.

**Example:**
- Client signs Growth: $1,000 setup + $499/month
- Associate earns $150 from setup
- Associate earns $99.80/month recurring for up to 12 months
- Year 1 total: $1,347.60
- After 12 months: $0/month on that client (unless they upgrade — new setup triggers a new commission)

---

### Level 3 — Certified 1APP Partner

**Best for:** A stronger seller who can also manage the relationship and possibly help with onboarding or basic setup after training.

**Requirements:**
- Complete 1APP training
- Bring in at least 3 paying clients
- Maintain CRM hygiene
- Demonstrate clean sales calls with no overpromising
- Get approval from Tony/Justin

**Responsibilities:**
- Prospect and close approved clients
- Help collect onboarding info
- Coordinate handoff to fulfilment
- Stay involved with client success when needed
- Protect retention by maintaining the relationship

**Compensation:**

| What | Rate |
|------|------|
| Setup fee commission | **20%** of setup fee collected |
| Monthly recurring | **20%** of MRR — **for the life of the client** (no cap) |

No 12-month cap at this level. As long as the client pays, you earn. This is the primary financial incentive to reach Level 3.

**Example — Same Growth deal at Level 3:**
- Client signs Growth: $1,000 setup + $499/month
- Associate earns $200 from setup
- Associate earns $99.80/month recurring — forever while client stays
- Year 1: $1,397.60 | Year 2: $1,197.60 | Year 3+: $1,197.60/yr
- A portfolio of 10 retained clients = ~$998/month in passive income

**Additional Level 3 Benefits:**

| Perk | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Lifetime recurring | No 12-month cap — earn as long as the client stays |
| Priority inbound leads | First access to inbound demo requests 1APP can’t take immediately |
| Override commission | Earn **5% of setup + 5% MRR** on sales from associates you personally recruited |
| Milestone bonuses | **$250 bonus** for every 5 active paying clients held simultaneously |
| Co-branding | “1APP Certified Partner” badge for LinkedIn, business card, and website |
| Direct support line | Direct access to Tony/Justin for deal strategy and close support |

These perks are active as long as the associate maintains Level 3 standing: no compliance violations and a minimum of 3 active paying clients at all times.

## 7. Team Building — Override Commission

Associates can build their own teams by recruiting other associates under them.

**When a team member you recruited closes a deal, you earn 5% of the total sale value — setup fee and monthly recurring — in addition to your own commissions.**

This is a single-level override. You earn on your direct recruits only, not on recruits that your recruits bring in.

### Override Rate

| What | Override Rate |
|------|--------------|
| Setup fee (any plan) | **5% of setup fee collected** |
| Monthly recurring (any plan) | **5% of MRR collected** |

Override commissions follow the same payment rules as direct commissions — paid on collected revenue only, reversed on refunds or chargebacks.

### Team Building Examples

**Example 1 — Your recruit closes a Growth deal:**
- Client pays $1,000 setup + $499/month
- Your recruit earns their commission (20% setup + 20% MRR)
- You earn: **$50 from setup** + **$24.95/month**

**Example 2 — Your recruit closes a Revenue Engine deal:**
- Client pays $1,500 setup + $799/month
- Your recruit earns their commission
- You earn: **$75 from setup** + **$39.95/month**

**Example 3 — You have 5 active recruits each closing 2 Growth deals/month:**
- 10 deals/month × $1,000 setup = $10,000 in setup revenue → you earn **$500**
- 10 clients × $499/month MRR = $4,990/month → you earn **$249.50/month**

### Team Override Summary

| Plan Closed by Your Recruit | Setup Override | Monthly Override |
|-----------------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Standard ($500 setup / $299/mo) | **$25** | **$14.95/mo** |
| Growth ($1,000 setup / $499/mo) | **$50** | **$24.95/mo** |
| Revenue Engine ($1,500 setup / $799/mo) | **$75** | **$39.95/mo** |

### Rules for Team Overrides

- Override applies to **direct recruits only** (one level deep)
- No override on your own sales — only on your team's sales
- The recruiting associate must be in good standing (active, no compliance violations)
- Override commissions are tracked by CRM source attribution
- If a team member is removed from the program, override on their accounts stops
- Team leaders are responsible for ensuring their recruits follow 1APP brand and conduct rules
- Recruiting associates who cause brand damage may lose override rights

### Team Leader Responsibilities

If you are building a team, you take on responsibility for:
- Ensuring your recruits complete required training before selling
- Coaching recruits on approved scripts and conduct rules
- Reporting conduct issues to Tony/Justin promptly
- Not making income claims to recruits that are not backed by real data

### Income Claim Rules

**Never promise recruits specific earnings.** The only honest statement is:

> Commissions depend entirely on how many qualified clients you close and retain. Results vary based on effort, market, and consistency.

Making exaggerated income claims to recruit associates is grounds for immediate removal from the program.

## 17. Recommended Starting Structure

For the first wave, keep it simple:

1. Everyone starts as **Referral Associate** or **Sales Associate**.
2. No one starts as Certified Partner.
3. Certified Partner status must be earned after proof of quality.
4. No overrides. No recruiting downline. No multi-level structure.
5. 1APP pays only on closed, paid, retained clients.

**Reason:** 1APP needs clean sales growth, not a messy army of people making claims the company has to clean up later.

---

## 17. Qualified Lead Rules

A lead is only commissionable if it is submitted properly and meets minimum fit.

**Minimum qualified lead:**
- Business owner or real decision-maker
- Located in a serviceable market
- Has a real business, not just an idea
- Has a phone/email/contact info
- Has one of these pains:
  - Missed calls
  - Poor follow-up
  - Manual booking/admin
  - Weak review collection
  - Lead leakage
  - Crew/job communication issues
- Agrees to a 1APP audit/demo or asks for pricing/info

**Not qualified:**
- Random names with no conversation
- People who cannot pay
- Businesses outside 1APP's current target market
- Duplicate leads already in 1APP CRM
- Leads submitted after another associate/company source already created the opportunity

---

## 17. Lead Ownership

**Lead protection window:** 90 days from CRM submission.

If the associate creates a qualified opportunity and logs it properly, they own commission rights for that lead for 90 days.

If there is no meaningful activity for 30 days, 1APP can reassign the lead.

If two associates claim the same lead:
1. First properly logged CRM record wins
2. If unclear, Tony/Justin decide
3. No arguing with the client or damaging the brand

## 17. Sales Process

### Step 1 — Identify
Find businesses that fit 1APP's target:
- Trades
- Home services
- Local appointment businesses
- Field-service companies
- Owner-operated companies doing roughly $250K–$3M/year

### Step 2 — Start Conversation
Use approved message/call script.

**Simple opener:**
> Quick question — if someone calls your business and you miss it while you're busy, does anything automatically follow up with them, or does it depend on someone calling back manually?

### Step 3 — Book Audit
CTA:
> We do a quick 15-minute audit and show you 3 places your business is losing time or leads. No pressure — if it's useful, we'll show you what 1APP would fix first.

### Step 4 — Discovery
Capture:
- Business name
- Contact name
- Phone/email
- Industry
- Current pain
- Current software/tools
- Number of leads/calls/jobs per week
- Biggest admin/follow-up problem
- Desired outcome

### Step 5 — Demo / Close
Tony/Justin or approved Sales Associate presents the right package.

### Step 6 — Handoff
After payment:
- Client onboarding form completed
- Account setup assigned
- Associate notes added to CRM
- Client gets clear launch timeline

## 17. Brand & Conduct Rules

Associates must:
- Use approved 1APP scripts and materials
- Be honest about capabilities
- Never guarantee revenue, leads, rankings, or specific results
- Never sell features 1APP has not approved
- Never use fake urgency or fake scarcity
- Never collect client payment personally unless explicitly authorized
- Never use 1APP branding on personal pages without approval
- Never represent themselves as an owner unless legally true
- Keep client/business information confidential

Violation can result in immediate removal from the program and forfeiture of unpaid commission tied to misconduct.

---

## 17. Payment Terms

Commissions are paid on **collected revenue only**.

**Payout timing:**
- Setup fee commission: paid after client payment clears and onboarding starts
- Monthly commission: paid monthly after client payment clears
- Refunds/chargebacks: commission is reversed or deducted from future payouts
- Taxes: associate is responsible for their own taxes
- Associates may need to invoice 1APP depending on final accounting setup

**No commission is paid on:**
- Taxes
- Payment processing fees
- Refunded amounts
- Chargebacks
- Discounts/free months unless approved
- Clients already in active 1APP pipeline

Before selling, each associate must complete:

## 17. Associate Agreement Terms to Include

This is not legal advice. The final agreement should include:

- Independent contractor status
- No employment relationship
- No authority to bind 1APP to contracts
- Approved use of brand/materials only
- Confidentiality
- Non-circumvention / no stealing clients
- Commission schedule (Standard Tier and Accelerated Tier)
- 50-client threshold definition and tracking method
- Payment timing
- Refund/chargeback clawback
- Lead ownership rules
- Termination rights
- No guaranteed income
- No guarantee of leads supplied by 1APP
- Client ownership belongs to 1APP
- Dispute process
- Governing law: Ontario, Canada

Have a lawyer/accountant review before scaling beyond close trusted associates.

---

## 17. Recommendation

Start with a controlled beta:

- **2–5 associates maximum**
- **30-day trial**
- **Referral Associate or Sales Associate only**
- **No Certified Partner status yet**
- **No custom pricing authority**

After 30 days, keep the people who produce clean opportunities and remove the rest.

The goal is not to have "a lot of people selling 1APP." The goal is to build a sales channel that brings in clients without creating brand damage, bad promises, or fulfilment headaches.
