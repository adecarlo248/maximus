## Current Website / Funnel Observations

### 1. The website already has WooCommerce

The site is built on WordPress + Divi + WooCommerce + WooCommerce Square.

This means Matt already has a basic online ordering layer, but it likely needs stronger automation around the order/customer journey.

### 2. Products are simple and clear

Current products found:
- 5 Cubic Yard Bin — $335 CAD
- 10 Cubic Yard Bin — $435 CAD
- Mattress/Box Spring Disposal — $35 CAD

This is good for automation because the offer is simple. 1APP does not need to explain a complicated product catalog.

### 3. The site CTA is split between phone and online ordering

Homepage pushes both:
- “Request a Bin” / phone call
- Product booking/order through WooCommerce

That creates two possible leakage points:
- People call and do not get answered
- People browse products but do not finish checkout/order

### 4. There is no obvious lead capture fallback

I did not find a clear contact form, quote request form, web chat, missed-call automation, or lead magnet/fallback for someone who is interested but not ready to order.

If someone has a question like “Can I put shingles in this?” or “Can you deliver to Lakefield?” they probably call. If that call is missed, the lead may be gone.

### 5. Review/social proof appears weak on-site

Product pages show “There are no reviews yet.”

For a local service business, reviews are a trust engine. 1APP can automate review requests after pickup/completed rental.

### 6. The checkout/cart path should be manually tested

My scripted add-to-cart test did not persist the cart. That may be bot/cookie protection, but it is worth manually checking live before/with Matt.

If the checkout has friction or fails for some users, that is a serious revenue leak.

## Best 1APP Angles For Matt

### Angle 1 — Missed Call Recovery

Bin rental is urgency-based. People often call when they are renovating, moving, cleaning out, or dealing with jobsite waste. If Red-E-Bins misses the call, the customer may call the next bin company.

1APP fix:
- Missed call text-back
- Instant SMS: “Sorry we missed you — are you looking to book a 5-yard or 10-yard bin?”
- Auto-follow-up if they do not reply
- Notify Matt/team instantly
- Push lead into pipeline

### Angle 2 — Bin Rental Lead Pipeline

Every inquiry should land in a simple pipeline:
- New inquiry
- Needs quote / question answered
- Bin requested
- Delivery scheduled
- Bin dropped off
- Pickup scheduled
- Completed
- Review requested

1APP fix:
- CRM pipeline
- Tags by bin size/location/job type
- Follow-up reminders
- Lost lead recovery
- No inquiry stuck in text messages or voicemail

### Angle 3 — Web Chat / Website Conversion

People visiting the site may have simple questions:
- Which bin size do I need?
- Do you deliver to my town?
- What can/can’t go in the bin?
- How long is the rental?
- What if I go over one tonne?

1APP fix:
- Website chat widget
- FAQ-style instant answers
- Capture name/phone/email
- Route hot leads to booking/order

### Angle 4 — Order Confirmation + Delivery Reminders

Once someone books/orders a bin, the operational workflow matters.

1APP fix:
- Confirmation SMS/email
- Delivery reminder
- Pickup reminder
- “Need an extra day?” follow-up
- “Anything prohibited in the bin?” reminder
- Internal notification for delivery/pickup schedule

### Angle 5 — Review Request Automation

After bin pickup, every happy customer should be asked for a review.

1APP fix:
- Automated Google review request after completed rental
- Follow-up if no review
- Route unhappy feedback privately before it becomes a bad public review

### Angle 6 — Abandoned Cart / Unfinished Booking Recovery

If someone adds a bin to cart but does not complete checkout, Red-E-Bins should follow up.

1APP fix:
- WooCommerce/Square order trigger if possible via integration
- Abandoned checkout follow-up
- “Still need a bin for your project?” SMS/email
- Manual task for Matt/team if automation cannot recover it directly

### Angle 7 — Location-Based Follow-Up

They serve multiple local areas. Leads can be tagged by area.

1APP fix:
- Tag leads by Peterborough, Lakefield, Norwood, Trent Lakes, etc.
- Track which areas produce the most inquiries
- Run seasonal campaigns by area
