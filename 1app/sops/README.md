# 1APP Standard Operating Procedures

**Version:** 1.2  
**Owner:** 1APP Technologies Inc.  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-22

This folder is the controlled operating system for selling, onboarding, launching, supporting, and retaining 1APP clients.

## Current Commercial Model

- Custom, quote-based pricing by automation.
- Monthly service starts as low as **$99**.
- Every approved free trial covers **one starter automation for 14 days**.
- No credit card is required to start the advertised trial.
- Starter trials use approved templates, target 1–3 business days after Ready-to-Build, include one revision round, and require no more than four hours of Tony's hands-on setup time.
- Advanced/custom work requires a paid setup project and separate delivery schedule.
- 1APP does not sell locked-in tiers.

## How To Use This Library

- Follow the master manual for process, ownership, standards, and escalation.
- Duplicate the launch checklist for every new client.
- Complete the handoff form before fulfillment begins.
- Review the operations scorecard every Monday with Tony and Justin.
- Do not edit a live checklist after launch. Create a new version and preserve the old record.

## Brand Rule

All documents, conversations, screenshots, training materials, and client-facing communications refer only to **1APP** and its named features. Underlying vendors or infrastructure are not part of the 1APP customer experience and must not be named.
