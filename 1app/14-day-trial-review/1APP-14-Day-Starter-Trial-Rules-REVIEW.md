# 1APP 14-Day Starter-Trial Rules — Review Draft

**Status:** Owner review required before website deployment or customer use  
**Date:** September 22, 2026

## Core Offer

- The 14-day free trial covers **one approved starter automation** designed to prove one measurable result.
- No credit card is required to start the advertised starter trial.
- The trial is not a free custom implementation.
- The trial clock begins only after the starter automation is live, tested, and usable.

## Starter-Trial Eligibility

An eligible starter trial must:

1. Use an approved 1APP template.
2. Target launch within **1–3 business days after Ready-to-Build**.
3. Require no more than **four hours of Tony's hands-on setup time**.
4. Include one agreed success measure.
5. Include one revision round.
6. Have a launch slot confirmed by Tony.

Typical eligible starters:

- Missed-call text-back.
- Booking confirmations and reminders.
- Basic website form or chat follow-up.
- Review-request automation.
- One simple new-lead follow-up sequence.
- One basic CRM pipeline using a proven template.

## Paid Implementation Required

The following are not free-trial builds and require a paid setup project with a separate delivery schedule:

- AI voice and advanced call flows.
- Custom integrations or API work.
- Accounting, estimate, invoice, or payment connections.
- Data cleanup, migration, or large imports.
- Custom websites or landing pages.
- Multiple locations, calendars, departments, or complex routing.
- Full community-platform implementation.
- Any material change outside the approved starter scope.

## Capacity Controls

- Maximum **four live trials** at one time.
- Maximum **two new trial launches per week**.
- Tony may approve a written exception after reviewing current paid-project and support workload.
- Sales may not promise a trial start date until Tony accepts the handoff and confirms a launch slot.

## Trial Process

1. Rod identifies one urgent, measurable problem.
2. The quote is marked **Starter Trial Eligible** or **Paid Implementation Required**.
3. Tony approves the scope, template, estimated setup hours, and launch slot.
4. The client completes onboarding and provides required secure access/content.
5. Tony builds and tests the approved starter automation.
6. The 14-day clock starts when Tony records the automation as live, tested, and usable.
7. Day 3 confirms activation and first value.
8. Day 7 reviews adoption and issues.
9. Day 10 schedules the conversion review and restates the approved paid price.
10. Day 12 confirms continue, paid modification, or end.
11. Day 14 converts after written authorization and cleared required payment, or the trial is offboarded.

## Client-Facing Website Wording

> Your 14-day free trial includes one approved starter automation designed to solve a specific business problem. No credit card is required. Starter trials normally launch within 1–3 business days after complete onboarding and access. The trial begins only when the starter automation is live, tested, and ready to use. AI voice, custom integrations, data migration, custom pages, multi-location routing, and expanded workflows are quoted separately. Trial availability is subject to current launch capacity.
