## 1. Business Overview

1APP helps small businesses stop losing leads by installing practical automation systems for missed calls, follow-up, booking, reminders, reviews, payments, and customer communication.

1APP is built for local service businesses that are too busy to answer every call, chase every lead, manually book every appointment, and remember every follow-up.

**Plain-English positioning:**

> 1APP helps busy local service businesses capture missed calls, follow up automatically, book more jobs, and save admin time without learning a complicated tech stack.

**Core message:**

> More leads do not help if the follow-up is broken. 1APP fixes the follow-up system.

## 12. 90-Day Success Framework

Because the LeadPRO Intermediate package is a 3-month commitment, success should be judged in phases.

### Month 1 — Setup + Validation

Focus:

- Campaign setup.
- Tracking validation.
- Landing page / CTA test.
- Keyword and audience testing.
- Negative keyword refinement.
- First lead quality assessment.

Main question:

> Are we attracting the right type of business owner?

### Month 2 — Optimization

Focus:

- Optimize toward booked audits and qualified leads.
- Cut weak keywords/audiences.
- Improve ad copy based on real search terms and lead quality.
- Improve landing page/CTA if clicks are not converting.

Main question:

> Which campaigns are producing qualified booked calls?

### Month 3 — Scale Decision

Focus:

- Scale winning keywords/audiences.
- Compare cost per booked audit against close rate.
- Compare customer acquisition cost against setup fee + monthly recurring revenue.
- Decide what to scale, pause, or rebuild.

Main question:

> Can we predictably acquire customers at a profitable cost?

## 14. Lead Handling Notes

Speed-to-lead is important.

Recommended lead handling:

- New form lead: contact within 5 minutes during business hours if possible.
- Booked audit: automated confirmation immediately.
- Reminder: 24-hour and 1-hour reminder.
- No-show: same-day follow-up.
- Unqualified lead: mark clearly in GHL with reason.

If possible, Page Pros should review lead-quality notes weekly so campaigns can be optimized based on sales feedback, not just platform metrics.
