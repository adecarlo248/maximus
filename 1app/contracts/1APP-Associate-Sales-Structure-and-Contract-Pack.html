1APP Associate Sales Structure + Contract Pack

Important: This pack is business/legal drafting support, not legal advice. 1APP should have an Ontario business lawyer review before sending to associates.

Advisor Note

Do not call these people employees unless 1APP is actually hiring them as employees.

The structure below is built for independent contractor associates:

  • Level 1: Referral Associate
  • Level 2: Sales Associate
  • Level 3: Certified Partner

If 1APP later hires a salaried or hourly salesperson, that needs a separate Ontario employment agreement covering ESA standards, payroll, vacation pay, termination entitlements, benefits, expenses, and employer obligations.

Cleaned Compensation Structure

Level 1 — Referral Associate

Best for people who can introduce warm business contacts but do not run sales calls.

Client PlanReferral Bonus
Standard$50
Growth$100
Revenue Engine$150

Rules:

  • Paid only when the client signs and pays their first invoice
  • No recurring commission
  • No pricing authority
  • No independent demos unless approved
  • No authority to make promises or bind 1APP

Level 2 — Sales Associate

Best for people who can prospect, qualify, follow up, book demos, and help close approved 1APP packages.

Compensation TypeRate
Setup fee commission15% of setup fee collected
Monthly recurring commission20% of MRR collected
Recurring cap12 paid months per client

Example on Growth plan:

  • Client pays $1,000 setup + $499/month
  • Associate earns $150 setup commission
  • Associate earns $99.80/month for up to 12 paid months
  • Year 1 potential: $1,347.60
  • After 12 months: recurring commission stops

Rules:

  • Must use approved scripts and pricing
  • Must track all activity in CRM
  • Cannot discount or customize terms without approval
  • Cannot promise results, lead volume, rankings, revenue, or launch timelines

Level 3 — Certified 1APP Partner

Best for proven sellers who can close cleanly, protect the brand, maintain CRM hygiene, and help with onboarding/client success.

Compensation TypeRate
Setup fee commission20% of setup fee collected
Monthly recurring commission20% of MRR collected
Recurring capNone — lifetime while client remains active and Partner remains eligible

Example on Growth plan:

  • Client pays $1,000 setup + $499/month
  • Partner earns $200 setup commission
  • Partner earns $99.80/month while client remains active and Partner remains eligible

Requirements:

  • Complete 1APP training
  • Bring in at least 3 paying clients, unless waived by 1APP
  • Maintain CRM hygiene
  • No brand/compliance issues
  • Approval from Tony/Justin or authorized 1APP leadership

Optional Team Override — Level 3 Only

Team-building rights should not be automatic.

If 1APP activates team-building rights in writing, a Level 3 Certified Partner may earn a single-level override on direct recruits only.

Override TypeRate
Setup fee override5% of setup fee collected
Monthly recurring override5% of MRR collected
DepthDirect recruits only — one level deep

Rules:

  • No override on the Partner’s own sales
  • No override beyond direct recruits
  • No income claims to recruits
  • 1APP can pause or remove override rights for brand/compliance risk
  • Best recommendation for first wave: do not activate overrides yet

Key Legal Protections Included in the Agreements

Each agreement includes:

  • Independent contractor status
  • No employment relationship
  • No authority to bind 1APP
  • Approved use of 1APP brand/materials only
  • Clear commission schedule
  • Payment on collected revenue only
  • Refund/chargeback clawback
  • CRM attribution rules
  • 90-day lead ownership window
  • 30-day inactivity reassignment rule
  • Client ownership belongs to 1APP
  • Confidentiality
  • Privacy and anti-spam compliance language
  • Non-circumvention / non-solicitation language
  • IP ownership protection
  • No guaranteed income or leads
  • Termination rights
  • Ontario governing law

Recommended Rollout

Start controlled:

  • 2–5 trusted people maximum
  • Level 1 or Level 2 only
  • 30-day trial
  • No Level 3 status at launch
  • No team overrides at launch
  • No custom pricing authority
  • All leads must go into CRM
  • All demos/closing calls reviewed until trust is earned

The goal is not “a lot of people selling 1APP.”

The goal is a clean sales channel that creates clients without creating brand damage, bad promises, or fulfillment chaos.
