1APP Sales Manager Hiring and Training Pack

Status: Working draft for owner approval and Ontario employment-law review Created: September 17, 2026 Company: 1APP Technologies Inc.

Purpose

This pack defines the first six months of Rod Norville's 1APP Sales Manager role, including compensation logic, team-production targets, team-building standards, product training, sales certification, reporting lines, access permissions, and the handoff from sales to fulfillment.

Current owner-approved compensation direction: one-time 10% personal commission on net collected setup fees plus 10% personal recurring commission from Rod's first qualifying personally sourced and closed client; 5% direct-team override on qualifying recurring revenue from direct recruits, with setup fees excluded; all future commission eligibility ends when Rod's employment eligibility period ends, subject to Ontario law; amounts already earned remain payable; regular pay dates are the 1st and 15th. The first-term target is 20 team-production paid-active clients and may include Rod's personal sales plus qualifying sales by eligible direct recruits.

Employment begins on the date the last party signs the agreement. Rod's first base-salary payment is due October 1, 2026, prorated from the Effective Date through September 30 if required. The exact Fixed-Term End Date must be inserted into the fully executed agreement as three calendar months after the Effective Date.

Legal structure selected by the owners

Use a three-month fixed-term employment agreement, not an independent-contractor agreement. The agreement expires at the stated End Date unless 1APP and Rod Norville sign a renewal or replacement agreement.

The role receives a fixed monthly salary, performs a core part of 1APP's business, manages and trains a company sales team, follows company systems, and is subject to company direction. Those facts strongly point toward employee status under Ontario's Employment Standards Act, regardless of what the agreement is called.

An Ontario employment lawyer must review the final agreement, especially the fixed-term and early-termination wording, renewal language, the Commission Eligibility Period, commission-on-termination, vacation, overtime/manager exemption, and fixed-target clauses.
