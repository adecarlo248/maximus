1APP Sales Certification and Scorecards

Certification rule

No Sales Manager or associate may independently sell, demo, quote, or recruit for 1APP until all required modules are complete and the certification owner records a pass.

Minimum passing score:

  • 80% on each written assessment.
  • 4/5 average on practical role-play.
  • No critical conduct failure.

A strong total score cannot offset a critical failure.

Module 1 — Company and offer

Candidate must explain:

  • What 1APP does in plain language.
  • Who it is for.
  • Custom per-automation quoting.
  • Starting price vs. final quote.
  • 14-day free trial and no-credit-card rule.
  • Difference between a trial activation and paid-active client.

Critical failures:

  • Says everything costs $99.
  • Guarantees a business result.
  • Requires a card to start the advertised trial.
  • Teaches old fixed tiers as the current offer.

Module 2 — Product and implementation

Candidate must explain for at least five automation categories:

  • Business problem solved.
  • Trigger and actions.
  • Required client information/access.
  • Human owner and escalation.
  • Stop conditions.
  • Trial success measure.
  • Key test cases.

Practical test: map one service-business problem into a safe, scoped automation recommendation.

Module 3 — ICP and qualification

Candidate must:

  • Identify good-fit and poor-fit prospects.
  • Ask Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-payoff questions.
  • Quantify pain without fabricating numbers.
  • Identify decision authority and timing.
  • Disqualify respectfully.

Module 4 — Demo and communication

Candidate must:

  • Set a mutual agenda.
  • Restate the prospect's pain.
  • Demonstrate only relevant workflows.
  • Explain outcomes rather than dumping features.
  • Handle questions without inventing technical answers.
  • Secure a dated next step.

Module 5 — Quote, trial, and close

Candidate must:

  • Build a line-item recommendation.
  • Explain scope, exclusions, setup requirements, and pricing.
  • Explain when the trial starts.
  • Define measurable trial success.
  • Explain paid conversion and billing authorization.
  • Distinguish a cleared setup-fee commission from paid-active status and recurring commission.
  • Respond to price, timing, trust, and “need to think” objections.

Module 6 — 1APP pipeline and CRM

Candidate must:

  • Create or update a clean contact and opportunity.
  • Record source and ownership.
  • Use correct stages and exit criteria.
  • Create a dated next action.
  • Avoid duplicates.
  • Produce a basic forecast based on evidence.

Module 7 — Handoff and onboarding

Candidate must complete a mock handoff with:

  • Decision-maker and contacts.
  • Exact business problem.
  • Selected automations, pricing, trial terms, success measure.
  • Services, hours, service area, booking and escalation rules.
  • Required access and integrations.
  • Approved AI knowledge and prohibited claims.
  • Client dependencies, launch date, and every promise made.

Critical failure: leaves a material promise or safety rule undocumented.

Module 8 — Conduct, privacy, and security

Candidate must know:

  • Approved claims and prohibited guarantees.
  • Secure access-sharing rules.
  • Consent and opt-out obligations.
  • No sharing client data.
  • No unauthorized discounts or contract changes.
  • No false ownership or earnings claims.
  • Incident escalation.

Any serious failure in this module is an automatic certification failure.

Module 9 — Computer, 1APP, AI, and onboarding practical

Using an approved laptop or desktop computer, the candidate must:

  • Join a video call, share the correct screen, and use a headset or private audio setup.
  • Sign in through an individual named account and complete multi-factor authentication.
  • Create a test contact and opportunity, add a note and task, and book a test appointment in the training account.
  • Find and complete the approved onboarding checklist.
  • Upload a non-secret sample asset to the approved location.
  • Use an approved AI tool or prompt to draft a client-facing message, identify one unsupported claim, and correct it.
  • Explain what client information must never be entered into an unapproved AI tool.
  • Recognize a task outside their permissions and escalate it correctly.

Passing standard: every task completed without coaching, no secret or real client data used, and no critical security error.

Critical failures:

  • Uses a shared or public computer for client work.
  • Shares a password, recovery code, or multi-factor-authentication code.
  • Enters confidential client data into an unapproved AI tool.
  • Attempts to bypass permissions or work inside a live client account without approval.

Sales call scorecard

Score each item 1–5.

  1. Preparation: researched business and likely problem.
  2. Opening: clear purpose, time, agenda, and permission to say no.
  3. Discovery: uncovers process and root problem.
  4. Implication: quantifies business impact.
  5. Listening: builds on answers and avoids interruption.
  6. Recommendation: smallest useful automation set.
  7. Value: ties each automation to stated pain/outcome.
  8. Accuracy: approved offer, pricing, trial, and claims.
  9. Objections: acknowledges, clarifies, responds, advances.
  10. Next step: mutual commitment with owner and date.

Passing standard: average 4/5 with no Accuracy score below 4.

Handoff scorecard

Each item is Pass/Fail:

  • Client identity and contacts complete.
  • Decision-maker confirmed.
  • Quote ID/version attached.
  • Selected automations named.
  • Setup fee, post-trial amount, taxes, and billing cadence recorded.
  • Trial scope, start trigger, end date, and success measures recorded.
  • Business problem and desired outcome recorded.
  • Scope and exclusions recorded.
  • Services, hours, service area, and emergency rules recorded.
  • Calendar, phone, messaging, AI, data, website, review, payment, and integration needs recorded where applicable.
  • Required access and client dependencies recorded.
  • Target launch date and reason recorded.
  • Every sales promise recorded.
  • Source, associate, and commission attribution recorded.

Passing standard: all applicable items pass.

Recruit interview scorecard

Score 1–5:

  • Professional communication.
  • Comfort speaking with business owners.
  • Coachability.
  • Follow-through history.
  • Ethical judgment.
  • General computer and AI literacy.
  • Ability to use 1APP and record activity accurately.
  • Reliable access to an approved laptop or desktop computer, stable internet, webcam or headset, and multi-factor-authentication method.
  • Ability to guide a client through the approved onboarding checklist by screen share.
  • Understanding of service-business pain.
  • Willingness to prospect and follow up.
  • Realistic earnings expectations.
  • Brand fit.

Automatic rejection:

  • Wants to sell before training.
  • Makes exaggerated income or product claims.
  • Refuses CRM use.
  • Wants to recruit a downline without selling or coaching.
  • Has a known record of mishandling client information or misrepresentation.
  • Does not have reliable access to the required computer, internet, and security tools.
  • Fails the computer, 1APP, responsible-AI, or onboarding practical.
  • Refuses multi-factor authentication or secure access rules.

First-three review

For every new associate, the Sales Manager must review:

  • First three prospecting conversations.
  • First three qualified opportunities.
  • First three audit/demo calls.
  • First three quotes or handoffs, where reached.

Independent activity continues only when the associate demonstrates safe, accurate performance.

Revenue

  • Paid-active clients vs. target.
  • New monthly recurring revenue.
  • Setup revenue collected.
  • Trial-to-paid conversions.
  • 30/60/90-day retention.

Pipeline

  • Qualified pipeline coverage.
  • Opportunities by stage.
  • Stage conversion.
  • Demos booked/showed.
  • Quotes sent/accepted.

  • Overdue next actions.

Recruiting

  • Candidates sourced.
  • Interviews completed.
  • Agreements signed.
  • Training started/completed.
  • Certifications passed.
  • Active Certified Sales Associates.
  • Associates active after 30 days.

Quality

  • CRM hygiene.
  • Handoff first-pass acceptance.
  • Call-review completion.
  • Unauthorized promise/discount incidents.
  • Client complaints.
  • Privacy/security issues.
  • Forecast accuracy.

Day-45 progress test

The manager should be at or above a credible trajectory to Day 90, supported by evidence—not optimism.

Review:

  • Valid quotes delivered and accepted, with quote-acceptance rate = accepted valid quotes ÷ valid quotes delivered.
  • Eligible 14-day trials completed and converted to paid-active, with trial-to-paid rate = paid-active conversions ÷ eligible completed trials.
  • Paid clients already converted, separated between Rod's personal sales and qualifying direct-team sales.
  • Trials that can realistically convert by the Fixed-Term End Date.
  • Qualified pipeline coverage.
  • Signed recruits in training.
  • Certified associates completing activity.
  • Bottlenecks, owners, and corrective actions.

Rod and the owners will confirm whether the 20-client production target still fits the actual conversion evidence and operating capacity. The target remains unchanged unless 1APP and Rod sign a written amendment. If the confirmed trajectory is insufficient, issue a written corrective plan with weekly checkpoints.

Fixed-Term End-Date evidence file

The Sales Manager submits:

  • List of 20 qualifying team-production paid-active clients, identifying Rod or the eligible direct recruit credited as source and closer, with opportunity IDs, attribution history, completed 14-day-trial evidence, and cleared-payment evidence.
  • List of 20 qualifying Active Certified Sales Associates with signed-agreement, training, certification, and activity evidence.
  • Revenue and commission reconciliation.
  • Client retention/risk list.
  • Pipeline forecast.
  • Training completion and call-review log.
  • Conduct, complaint, and handoff-quality report.
  • Proposed Days 91–180 plan.
