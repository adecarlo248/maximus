## 1. Position and reporting

The Company employs the Employee as **Sales Manager** with the following functional reporting structure:

- **Tony DeCarlo:** technical questions, implementation feasibility, client setup, security, access, fulfillment, and technical escalation.
- **Justin Roffey:** sales strategy, business development, recruiting, pricing within approved authority, pipeline management, commercial coaching, and business escalation.

Any decision that changes both commercial scope and technical delivery requires written approval from both Tony DeCarlo and Justin Roffey. Neither reporting path authorizes the Employee to exceed the written authority in this Agreement or Company policy.

The Employee will faithfully perform the duties in this Agreement, the attached schedules, and reasonable duties consistent with the position. The Employee has no authority to bind the Company, vary client terms, hire or dismiss personnel, incur expenses, or make commitments unless that authority is provided in writing.

## 2. Three-month fixed-term employment

Employment begins on the Effective Date, meaning the date on which both parties have signed and the last signature is applied. Employment ends on the Fixed-Term End Date, subject to any earlier termination provision approved by Ontario employment counsel.

The parties intend this as fixed-term employment, not an independent-contractor relationship. The term does not remove or reduce any right or minimum standard under applicable law.

There is no automatic renewal, continued employment, promotion, or compensation change. Before the Fixed-Term End Date, the parties may review results and negotiate a renewal or replacement agreement. Any continuation must be confirmed in a written agreement signed by the Company and Rod Norville.

## 3. Duties

The Employee's primary duties include:

1. Build, lead, coach, and manage the 1APP sales team.
2. Recruit suitable sales associates through lawful, approved methods.
3. Deliver and maintain 1APP sales onboarding, product training, certification, role-play, and ongoing coaching.
4. Ensure no associate sells independently before certification and approval.
5. Prospect, qualify, demonstrate, quote, follow up, negotiate, and close approved 1APP opportunities.
6. Ensure every lead, contact, opportunity, activity, next action, quote, and source is accurately recorded in 1APP.
7. Maintain accurate weekly forecasts and performance reports.
8. Enforce approved offers, pricing controls, claim restrictions, privacy, consent, and brand standards.
9. Submit complete sales-to-fulfillment handoffs before implementation begins.
10. Coordinate with implementation and customer-success owners so sales promises match deliverable scope.
11. Review calls, coach performance, address poor conduct, and escalate serious issues.
12. Protect client experience, retention, recurring revenue, and Company reputation.

The Employee will follow the 1APP Master SOP Manual, Sales Manager Training Manual, Certification and Scorecards, privacy/security rules, and written policies as amended from time to time, provided changes comply with applicable law and do not unlawfully reduce contractual entitlements.

## 5. Base salary

The Company will pay the Employee **$5,000 CAD gross per month** through payroll and subject to statutory deductions, for a total scheduled base salary of **$15,000 CAD over the three-month fixed term**, subject to lawful payroll timing, deductions, and any approved early-termination provision.

The Employee's **first base-salary payment is due October 1, 2026**. It will cover the period from the Effective Date through September 30, 2026 and will be lawfully prorated if that is less than a full semi-monthly pay period. After that initial payment, regular pay dates are the **1st and 15th of each month**. The scheduled full semi-monthly base payment is **$2,500 CAD gross**, subject to lawful proration, deductions, vacation-pay treatment, and payroll adjustments. If a later scheduled date is not a banking or payroll business day, payment will be made on the Company's next lawful payroll business day.

## 6. Commission and incentive compensation

The Employee may earn commission under **Schedule A — Sales Manager Compensation and KPI Plan**, as approved and signed by both parties.

Commission is governed by the written schedule in effect for the applicable deal, subject to minimum employment standards. The Company will provide a calculation record with each commission payment. No manager or owner may change commission retroactively after it has been earned.

Beginning with the Employee's **first qualifying personally sourced and personally closed client**, the Employee earns a **one-time commission equal to 10% of the net collected setup fee** actually received and cleared by the Company during the Employee's Commission Eligibility Period under Schedule A. The setup-fee commission is earned as each approved setup-fee installment clears. It is not recurring, does not vest as a lifetime right, and does not apply to invoiced but uncollected amounts, HST, refunds, credits, chargebacks, reimbursed expenses, usage pass-through, or third-party costs.

Beginning with the Employee's **first qualifying personally sourced and personally closed paid-active client**, the Employee also earns **10% of net collected monthly recurring service revenue** received and cleared during the Employee's Commission Eligibility Period under Schedule A.

All setup-fee commission, recurring commission, and team override eligibility is employment-dependent and does not vest as a lifetime or post-employment right. Subject to applicable law, the **Commission Eligibility Period** ends on the Employee's last day of active employment, including any additional period during which commission eligibility must continue under Ontario employment-standards legislation. No new commission accrues on setup-fee or subscription revenue received after the Commission Eligibility Period. Amounts already earned before that period ends remain payable on the next applicable pay date after reconciliation.

The Employee also earns a **5% direct-team override** on net collected monthly recurring service revenue received and cleared during the Commission Eligibility Period from qualifying clients personally sourced and personally closed by an eligible sales associate whom the Employee directly recruited, as defined in Schedule A. The override does not apply to setup fees. Qualifying team clients count toward the Employee's production target under Section 9 and Schedule A, but do not create a 10% personal commission for the Employee. The override does not stack with the Employee's 10% personal-client recurring commission on the same client revenue.

For clarity, no commission is earned merely because a quote is sent, a client verbally agrees, an invoice is issued, or a trial starts. The one-time personal setup-fee commission is earned only after the approved setup-fee payment clears during the Commission Eligibility Period and the attribution, quote, handoff, and conduct requirements in Schedule A are satisfied. Personal recurring commission and the direct-team override are earned only after the **14-day trial** is complete, the client authorizes paid continuation, and the applicable recurring client payment clears during the Commission Eligibility Period. Earned commission and override amounts are paid on the **1st and 15th** under the reconciliation rules in Schedule A, subject to applicable law.

## 8. Expenses and Company property

## 9. Performance expectations

The Employee agrees to the first-term standards in Schedule A. Performance will be judged on both output and quality. The first-term production target is **20 qualifying Net-New Paid-Active Clients produced by Rod Norville and/or eligible sales associates whom Rod directly recruited and manages**. Each client may be counted once only. Owner sales and sales by people outside Rod's direct eligible team do not count unless approved in a written amendment before the sale is credited.

At approximately Day 45, the Company and Employee will conduct a formal target-fit review using actual quote-acceptance and trial-to-paid conversion data, including the underlying counts and denominators. They will review pipeline coverage, trial volume, sales capacity, fulfillment capacity, and client quality. The review confirms whether the 20-client target remains commercially reasonable; it does not automatically change the target. Any revised target must be recorded in a written amendment signed by the Company and Employee.

The Days 91–180 promotion-eligibility standards apply only if the parties sign a renewal or replacement agreement that expressly adopts them.

Targets are management expectations, not permission to use misleading claims, pressure tactics, hidden activity, unauthorized pricing, improper recruiting, privacy violations, or incomplete handoffs.

Failure to meet a target may result in coaching, a performance-improvement plan, changed responsibilities, loss of promotion eligibility, or other lawful action. It does not automatically determine legal cause for termination.

## 10. Sales and recruiting conduct

The Employee must not, and must ensure direct reports do not:

- Guarantee leads, revenue, rankings, savings, or results.
- Misstate that $99/month applies to every client or automation package.
- Require a credit card to start the advertised 14-day free trial.
- Invent discounts, pricing, features, integrations, timelines, or contract terms.
- Describe themselves as an owner, partner, legal agent, or authorized signer unless formally appointed.
- Make earnings guarantees or misleading recruiting claims.
- Operate an unapproved multi-level recruiting structure.
- Handle passwords, payment credentials, or client data outside approved secure systems.
- Keep leads or client communication outside 1APP.

## 14. Conflicts, outside activities, and non-solicitation

The Employee must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest and may not use Company time, leads, information, or opportunities for another business.

Any post-employment non-solicitation restriction must be narrowly drafted and approved by Ontario counsel before this Agreement is signed. No non-competition restriction is intended unless counsel confirms it is lawful and appropriate.
