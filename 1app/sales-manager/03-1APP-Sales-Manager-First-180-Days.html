1APP Sales Manager — Initial Three-Month Term and Conditional Months 4–6

Mission

Build a disciplined sales team that can consistently identify service-business pain, sell the smallest useful 1APP automation set, create clean handoffs, and convert trials into retained paid clients without overpromising.

Outcomes

By Day 30

  • Sales Manager passes all 1APP certifications.
  • Recruiting funnel is live.
  • First associate cohort is recruited and in training.
  • Every lead and recruit is tracked in 1APP.
  • Approved pitch, discovery, demo, quote, follow-up, and handoff process is in use.
  • Weekly sales meeting, pipeline review, call coaching, and reporting cadence are operating.

By Day 60

  • At least 20 associates are signed or in certification, with enough volume to produce 20 Active Certified Sales Associates by the Fixed-Term End Date.
  • Client pipeline is at least three times the remaining paid-client target.
  • Early trials have clear success measures and conversion review dates.
  • First three calls and opportunities for each new associate are reviewed.
  • Conversion bottlenecks have named corrective actions.

By the Fixed-Term End Date

  • Rod's team produces 20 Net-New Paid-Active Clients through any combination of Rod's personal sales and qualifying sales by eligible associates whom Rod directly recruited and manages.
  • 20 Active Certified Sales Associates.
  • CRM, handoff, conduct, and training quality gates are met.
  • Day-90 commercial and role review is complete.

Conditional Days 91–180

These targets apply only if 1APP and Rod Norville sign a renewal or replacement agreement that expressly adopts them.

  • Rod's team produces 30 additional Net-New Paid-Active Clients through any combination of Rod's personal sales and qualifying sales by eligible associates on Rod's direct team.
  • Improve stage conversion and forecast accuracy.
  • Retain at least 80% of first-period paid clients, subject to approved exceptions.
  • Maintain at least 15 active certified associates.
  • Document a repeatable manager-led recruiting and coaching system.
  • Become eligible for consideration for Director of Sales; the promotion is not automatic and requires owner approval and a signed amendment.

13-week execution plan

Week 1 — Learn and prove foundation

  • Complete company, offer, product, buyer, conduct, privacy, and SOP training.
  • Demonstrate the 30-second, 2-minute, and discovery-led 1APP pitch.
  • Map the full opportunity pipeline.
  • Review current clients, wins, losses, and actual automations delivered.
  • Set up personal scorecard and calendar.

Week 2 — Learn fulfillment before recruiting sellers

  • Shadow onboarding and implementation.
  • Complete one mock sales-to-fulfillment handoff.
  • Trace an automation from quote through trial, testing, launch, and paid conversion.
  • Pass setup/onboarding knowledge test.
  • Finalize associate recruiting funnel and interview scorecard.

Week 3 — Launch recruiting and selling

  • Start daily prospecting and recruiting blocks.
  • Interview first associate candidates.
  • Run first supervised audits/demos.
  • Hold first role-play clinic.
  • Publish first forecast with evidence-based stages.

Week 4 — Certify first cohort

  • Complete cohort training.
  • Certify only people who pass product, CRM, discovery, conduct, and handoff standards.
  • Review every active deal.
  • Correct stale or unsupported opportunities.

Weeks 5–6 — Coach real activity and validate the target

  • Each certified associate begins approved prospecting.
  • Manager reviews the first three opportunities and first three calls per associate.
  • Run two role-play sessions per week.
  • Review speed-to-lead, show rate, quote rate, trial starts, and blockers.
  • Day-45 formal target-fit review using actual quote-acceptance and 14-day trial-to-paid counts and rates. Confirm the target or document any change in a signed amendment.

Weeks 7–8 — Build pipeline coverage

  • Maintain at least three times the remaining client target in qualified pipeline.
  • Remove weak or fake pipeline.
  • Tighten discovery and quote quality.
  • Make trial success measures explicit before launch.
  • Add remedial training based on real call defects.

Weeks 9–10 — Convert trials and protect quality

  • Run structured Day 7, Day 10, Day 12, and Day 14 trial reviews.
  • Ensure no trial is counted as a paid client.
  • Inspect handoff defects, cancellations, and client complaints.
  • Coach managers and associates on closing without unsupported promises.

Weeks 11–12 — Close target gap

  • Create a named mutual action plan for every realistic Day-90 deal.
  • Focus on high-fit prospects and remove vanity activity.
  • Audit all recruit definitions and evidence.
  • Prepare Day-90 performance file.

Week 13 — Fixed-term review and renegotiation

  • Reconcile paid-active clients and active certified associates.
  • Review revenue, retention risk, commissions, conduct, and CRM quality.
  • Document lessons and update training.
  • Decide whether to end the relationship at the Fixed-Term End Date or sign a renewal/replacement agreement with the Days 91–180 plan in writing.

Weekly operating rhythm

Monday — Numbers and commitments

  • Funnel by stage.
  • Remaining gap to paid-client target.
  • Recruiting funnel and certification status.
  • Top deals, blockers, next actions.
  • Capacity and handoff risks.

Tuesday — Skill training

  • 30–45 minute product or sales module.
  • Role-play using live scenarios.
  • One scored competency per week.

Wednesday — Call and deal coaching

  • Review recordings or documented calls.
  • Inspect discovery, value mapping, objections, and next steps.
  • Review incomplete handoffs with fulfillment.

Thursday — Recruiting and activation

  • Candidate interviews.
  • New-associate onboarding.
  • First-call and first-opportunity support.

Friday — Forecast and learning

  • Forecast paid conversions, not verbal promises.
  • Review wins, losses, trial results, and source quality.
  • Record one process failure and one corrective action.
  • Submit owner report.

Required owner report

  • Paid-active clients this week/month/period.
  • Trials live and expected conversion dates.
  • Collected setup revenue and new monthly recurring revenue.
  • Active certified associates and candidates by stage.
  • Activity and stage conversion by associate.
  • At-risk deals and clients.
  • Handoff defects and conduct issues.
  • Decisions needed from Tony/Justin.
