1APP Associate Sales Program

A clean, controlled structure for people who want to sell 1APP and earn recurring commissions — without turning the business into chaos.

1. Program Positioning

1APP Associates are independent sales/referral partners who introduce local businesses to 1APP and help book qualified audits and demos. Associates are not employees, not owners, and not authorized to make promises outside approved 1APP offers.

Associates bring in qualified clients. 1APP owns the brand, pricing, client accounts, fulfilment standards, and final approval of every deal.

Plain-English pitch for associates:
"1APP helps small businesses stop losing leads, automate follow-up, book more jobs, and save time using one simple business app. Your job is to start conversations with business owners, book qualified audits, and help them see where they're leaking time or money."

2. Best-Fit Associate Profile

Ideal Associates:
  • Connected locally — knows business owners
  • Comfortable on the phone, DM, text, in-person
  • Coachable and follows the system
  • Not afraid of consistent follow-up
  • Understands small business pain
  • Represents the brand professionally
  • Uses the approved pitch — not making random claims
Avoid:
  • Only want passive referral money
  • Exaggerate income claims to recruits
  • Discount pricing without permission
  • Can't follow a simple CRM process
  • Could damage the 1APP brand for a quick commission

4. Associate Levels

Best for people who can introduce warm business contacts but do not want to run full sales calls.

  • Promise features, timelines, or results
  • Run demos (unless approved)
  • Claim to be an owner/partner of 1APP

7. Team Building — Override Commission

Associates can build their own teams by recruiting other associates. When a team member you recruited closes a deal, you earn 5% of the total sale value — setup fee and monthly recurring — on top of your own commissions.

This is a single-level override. You earn on your direct recruits only — not on recruits that your recruits bring in.

Team Override Rules

What counts:
  • Direct recruits only — one level deep
  • Override on setup fee and MRR for all their closed accounts
  • Tracked by CRM source attribution
  • Paid monthly alongside your own commissions
What does not count:
  • Your own personal sales (override is team-only)
  • Recruits who are not in good standing
  • Accounts on refund or chargeback
  • Recruits that your recruits bring in (not multi-level)

Income Claim Rule — Non-Negotiable

Never promise recruits specific earnings. The only honest statement is:

"Commissions depend entirely on how many qualified clients you close and retain. Results vary based on effort, market, and consistency."

Making exaggerated income claims to recruit associates is grounds for immediate removal and forfeiture of all unpaid commissions.

8. Recommended Starting Structure

Start with a controlled beta: 2–5 associates maximum, 30-day trial, Referral or Sales Associate only. Keep the people who produce clean opportunities. Remove the rest.
For the first wave:
  • 2–5 associates max
  • 30-day trial period
  • Level 1 or Level 2 only to start
  • No Certified Partner status yet
  • No overrides or downline recruiting yet
  • No custom pricing authority
  • Tony/Justin review every deal before it closes
After 30 days, promote who:
  • Submitted clean leads consistently
  • Followed the scripts and process
  • Maintained CRM hygiene
  • Represented the brand properly
  • Showed consistent activity and follow-up

The goal is not to have a lot of people selling 1APP. The goal is a clean channel that brings in clients without brand damage, bad promises, or fulfilment headaches.

9. Qualified Lead Rules

Minimum Qualified Lead:
  • Business owner or real decision-maker
  • Located in a serviceable market
  • Has a real, operating business
  • Has phone/email/contact info
  • Has at least one core pain point (missed calls, poor follow-up, weak reviews, manual booking, lead leakage)
  • Agrees to a 1APP audit/demo
Not Qualified:
  • Random names with no real conversation
  • Businesses that can't afford the service
  • Outside 1APP's target market
  • Duplicates already in 1APP CRM
  • Leads submitted after another source created the opportunity

Lead protection window: 90 days from CRM submission. First properly logged CRM record wins on disputes.

10. Lead Ownership

  • All clients belong to 1APP — not the associate
  • Associates cannot take clients to competitors during or after the program
  • Commission attribution follows the first properly logged CRM submission
  • Non-circumvention applies for 24 months after leaving the program
  • Violation = immediate removal and forfeiture of all commissions

11. Sales Process

StepWhat To Do
1. IdentifyFind trades, home services, local appointment businesses, field-service companies doing $250K–$3M/year where missed calls or admin pain costs real money.
2. Start Conversation"Quick question — if someone calls your business and you miss it while you're busy on a job, does anything automatically follow up, or does it depend on someone calling back manually?"
3. Book Audit"We do a quick 15-minute audit and show you 3 places your business is losing time or leads. No pressure — if it's useful, we'll show you what 1APP would fix first."
4. DiscoveryCapture: business name, contact, phone/email, industry, pain, current tools, leads/calls/week, admin problem, desired outcome.
5. Demo / CloseTony/Justin or approved Sales Associate presents the right package. Bring 1APP in on the close when needed.
6. HandoffAfter payment: onboarding form completed, account setup assigned, associate notes added to CRM, client gets launch timeline.

12. Brand & Conduct Rules

Associates must:
  • Use approved 1APP scripts and materials
  • Be honest about capabilities and timelines
  • Keep client/business info confidential
  • Track every lead and activity in CRM
  • Identify themselves as an independent associate — not an employee or owner
Associates cannot:
  • Guarantee revenue, leads, rankings, or results
  • Discount pricing without approval
  • Collect client payment personally
  • Claim to be an owner or partner of 1APP
  • Use unapproved 1APP branding or materials
  • Use fake urgency or fake scarcity

Violation = immediate removal from the program and forfeiture of unpaid commissions tied to misconduct.

13. Payment Terms

All commissions are paid on collected revenue only.

When commissions are paid:
  • Setup fee commission — after client payment clears and onboarding starts
  • Monthly commission — monthly after client payment clears
No commission is paid on:
  • Refunded amounts or chargebacks
  • Payment processing fees
  • Discounts or free months
  • Clients already in active 1APP pipeline before your submission

Associates are independent contractors and responsible for their own taxes. Invoicing 1APP may be required depending on final accounting setup.

14. Required Training Before Selling

Every associate must complete the following before selling:

  1. 1APP product overview
  2. Target customer training
  3. Offer and package training
  4. Approved scripts and openers
  5. Qualification checklist
  1. CRM lead submission process
  2. Compliance and brand rules
  3. Objection handling
  4. Demo handoff process
  5. Compensation rules and payment terms
Certification test: Associate must role-play one outbound conversation and one audit booking before being allowed to sell independently.

17. Recommendation

Start Controlled. Scale What Works.

Pick 2–5 people you already trust. Run a 30-day beta. Keep the ones who follow the system and produce clean opportunities. Promote them to Level 2. Watch who consistently closes and retains — those are your Level 3s.

The goal is not to build a big team of associates. The goal is a clean, lean sales channel that brings in clients without brand damage, broken promises, or fulfilment headaches.

Every great associate you bring in multiplies your reach without multiplying your problems. Every bad one creates the exact problems this program is designed to prevent.
