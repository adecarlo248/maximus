# 1APP Associate Quickstart — One-Pager v2

## What Associates Sell

1APP helps small businesses stop losing leads, automate follow-up, book more jobs, collect reviews, and save admin time using one simple business app.

---

## Who To Target

Best targets:
- Trades
- Home services
- Field-service companies
- Local appointment businesses
- Owner-operated companies doing roughly $250K–$3M/year

Listen for:
- "We miss calls."
- "I forget to follow up."
- "Booking is messy."
- "Customers don't answer."
- "I'm doing too much admin."

## Associate Levels

### Referral Associate
- Makes warm introductions and books business owners into an audit/demo
- Does not run demos or quote custom pricing
- **Earns:** Flat referral bonus per plan signed

### Sales Associate (Level 2)
- Prospects, qualifies, follows up, and helps close
- Uses approved scripts and standard pricing only
- Tracks every lead in CRM
- **Earns:** 15% setup + 20% MRR (12-month cap per client)

### Certified 1APP Partner (Level 3)
- Earned after proof — not given upfront
- Minimum 3 paying clients, clean CRM, approved by Tony/Justin
- **Earns:** 20% setup + 20% MRR — **lifetime, no cap**
- **Plus:** Priority inbound leads, override on your recruits (5%), milestone bonuses ($250/5 clients), co-branding rights, direct Tony/Justin access

## Rules

Associates cannot:
- Promise guaranteed revenue, leads, or results
- Discount without approval
- Sell custom builds without approval
- Claim to be an owner/partner unless legally true
- Collect client payment personally unless authorized
- Use unapproved marketing materials

## Qualified Lead Info To Capture

- Business name
- Contact name
- Phone/email
- Industry
- Pain/problem
- Current system/tools
- Desired outcome
- Audit/demo date booked
- Notes from conversation

## Main Rule

Do not overpromise. Book qualified audits, track everything in CRM, and let 1APP close and fulfil cleanly.
