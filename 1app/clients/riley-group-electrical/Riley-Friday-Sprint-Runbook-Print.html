6. How to price a verified estimate

Price the value of a verified estimate opportunity, not a booking and not a guaranteed sale. Use the selected project category's real history—not Riley's highest-value job or a company-wide guess.

  1. Average collected job value: actual revenue collected from completed jobs in the selected category.
  2. Gross margin: collected revenue minus direct labour, material and subcontractor costs, divided by collected revenue.
  3. Estimate-to-sale close rate: sold jobs divided by written estimates for that category and a recent comparable period.
Expected gross profit per estimate = average collected job value × gross margin × close rate
Raw 1APP fee = expected gross profit per estimate × 20%

Round the raw fee to the nearest $25, then charge the lower of that amount or the standard price-band ceiling.

7. Billable verified estimate

All conditions must be true: approved cohort; positive engagement within the attribution period; written qualification met; appointment kept; dated written estimate issued within the proof window; opportunity not active before launch.

Not billable: booking alone, no-show, cancellation, duplicate, active job, open estimate, complaint, emergency, excluded small service call, unqualified prospect or undocumented estimate.

Client explanation: “The fee is for creating a verified estimate opportunity from a dormant contact. Riley still controls pricing, availability, sales process and closing, which is why the fee is set as a limited fraction of the expected gross profit per estimate.”
