## Proposed billable event

A verified estimate is billable only when all of the following are true:

- The contact came from the client-approved campaign cohort.
- The person positively replied and met the written service, area and minimum-value qualifications.
- The appointment was kept.
- Riley issued a dated written estimate tied to that customer within the agreed proof window.
- The opportunity was not already active before the campaign.

Not billable: bookings alone, no-shows, duplicates, current jobs, existing open estimates, unqualified inquiries, small service calls excluded by the agreement, opt-outs, or undocumented estimates.
