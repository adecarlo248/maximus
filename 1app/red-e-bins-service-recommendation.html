The Simple Version

Red-E-Bins already has the core foundation: a clear local service, simple bin sizes, online ordering, and a strong Peterborough/Kawartha service area.

The main opportunity is not “more software.” It is making sure every call, website visitor, inquiry, order, delivery, pickup, and review request is followed up automatically.

What 1APP Would Fix First

Missed calls

If someone calls while Matt is busy and no one answers, they may call the next bin rental company. 1APP sends an instant text back so the lead is not lost.

Website questions

Visitors often need simple answers before booking: bin size, delivery area, rental length, weight, or prohibited items. A website chat/intake form captures them.

Booking/order follow-up

Customers get confirmation, delivery reminders, pickup reminders, and clear instructions without Matt manually chasing every detail.

Google reviews

After pickup, happy customers are automatically asked for a Google review. This builds trust and helps future customers choose Red-E-Bins.
