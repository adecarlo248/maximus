# 1APP — 30-Day Client Acquisition Business Plan

## Core Target

**Goal:** 10 new paying clients every 30 days.

**Primary offer:** 1APP helps small businesses get their time back by automating missed calls, follow-up, booking, reminders, client communication, lead nurture, reviews, and operational workflows.

**Positioning line:**
> 1APP gives busy small businesses one simple app to capture leads, follow up automatically, book jobs, and save hours every week.

**Plain-English pitch:**
> Most small businesses are losing time and money because calls, follow-ups, estimates, reminders, and admin tasks are still being handled manually. 1APP fixes that by installing simple automation systems that keep the business moving even when the owner is busy.

## 2. Chamber of Commerce / Local Business Lists

### Important Compliance Note

Do not assume a Chamber list can be blasted by email.

Before sending campaigns, confirm:
- Did the businesses consent to receive marketing emails?
- Is the list public or member-only?
- Are there Chamber rules about solicitation?
- Does the list include role-based emails like info@, admin@, sales@?
- Are you operating under Canadian CASL requirements?

### Safer Use of Chamber Lists

Use Chamber lists for:
- Prospect research
- Building a call list
- Finding business categories
- Direct mail
- Manual personalized outreach
- LinkedIn/Facebook research
- Warm introductions through Justin

Email can be used carefully when there is a legitimate business context, but every message should be personalized, relevant, include business identity, and include an unsubscribe path.

## 9. Biggest Risks

### Risk 1 — Too broad
If 1APP targets every small business, the message gets weak.

**Recommendation:** Start with contractors/trades first.

### Risk 2 — Selling “AI” instead of outcomes
Most business owners don’t care about AI. They care about saving time, getting more jobs, and not missing leads.

**Recommendation:** Lead with time back, missed leads, follow-up, booking, and crew/admin pain.

### Risk 3 — Email compliance/list quality
Chamber lists may not be legal or effective for bulk cold email.

**Recommendation:** Use them for research and personalized outreach first. Confirm CASL/compliance before bulk campaigns.

### Risk 4 — Building too much before selling
Crew Clock is valuable, but only if buyers confirm they’ll pay for it.

**Recommendation:** Sell the audit and manually validate demand before overbuilding.
