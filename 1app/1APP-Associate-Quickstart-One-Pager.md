# 1APP Associate Quickstart — One-Pager

## What Associates Sell

1APP helps small businesses stop losing leads, automate follow-up, book more jobs, collect reviews, and save admin time using one simple business app.

## Who To Target

Best targets:
- Trades
- Home services
- Field-service companies
- Local appointment businesses
- Owner-operated companies doing roughly $250K–$3M/year

Listen for:
- “We miss calls.”
- “I forget to follow up.”
- “Booking is messy.”
- “Customers don’t answer.”
- “I’m doing too much admin.”
- “My crew/job communication is a mess.”

## First Message / Opener

> Quick question — if someone calls your business and you miss it while you’re busy, does anything automatically follow up with them, or does it depend on someone calling back manually?

If they say manual / no / not really:

> That’s exactly what 1APP helps fix. We set up simple automations so missed calls, follow-up, booking, reminders, and reviews don’t fall through the cracks. Want me to book you a quick 15-minute audit so we can show you 3 places your business is leaking time or leads?

## CTA

Book a **Free 15-Minute Time-Saving Audit**.

Promise:

> We’ll show you 3 places your business is wasting time or losing leads — and what automation would fix first.

## Associate Levels

### Referral Associate
- Makes warm introductions
- Books business owners into audit/demo
- Does not quote custom pricing or run demos
- **Commission:** $150 when referred client signs and pays first invoice

### Sales Associate
- Prospects, qualifies, follows up, and helps close
- Uses approved scripts and standard pricing
- Tracks every lead in CRM
- **Commission:** 40% of setup fee + 20% MRR for first 12 paid months

### Certified 1APP Partner
- Earned after proof, not given upfront
- Can sell at a higher level and help manage client relationship
- **Option A:** 50% setup + 25% MRR for first 12 paid months
- **Option B:** 50% setup + 15% ongoing MRR if actively managing retention/support

## Rules

Associates cannot:
- Promise guaranteed revenue/leads/results
- Discount without approval
- Sell custom builds without approval
- Claim to be an owner/partner unless legally true
- Collect payment personally unless authorized
- Use random/unapproved marketing materials

## Qualified Lead Info To Capture

- Business name
- Contact name
- Phone/email
- Industry
- Pain/problem
- Current system/tools
- Desired outcome
- Audit/demo date booked
- Notes from conversation

## Packages To Present

### Starter — $199/month
Missed call text-back, booking calendar, reminders, basic follow-up, simple pipeline.

### Growth — $399/month
Starter plus AI/chat or voice intake where approved, estimate/invoice follow-up, review requests, reactivation, monthly summary.

### Pro Ops — $599+/month
Growth plus crew/job workflows, Crew Clock/time-tracking workflow where applicable, custom automation, advanced reporting, priority support.

## Main Rule

Do not overpromise. Book qualified audits, track everything, and let 1APP close/fulfil cleanly.
