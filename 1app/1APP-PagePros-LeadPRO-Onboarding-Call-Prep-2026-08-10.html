1. The Hard Truth

Page Pros can drive traffic, but they cannot fix unclear targeting or a weak conversion path. The onboarding call should not be “tell us what you do.” It should be: “Here is exactly who we sell to, what pain converts, what counts as a qualified lead, what assets you can use, and what results we need measured.”

Your job on the call is to force clarity on:

  • Who they are targeting.
  • What offer the ads are pushing.
  • What landing page/form/booking link they are sending traffic to.
  • What counts as a qualified lead.
  • How every lead, booking, call, form fill, and paid customer will be tracked.

17. Recommended 90-Day Success Metrics

Because the package requires a 3-month commitment, judge it in phases instead of panicking after week two.

Month 1: setup, campaign launch, tracking validation, keyword/negative keyword refinement, first lead-quality read.

Month 2: optimize toward booked audits and qualified leads; cut weak keywords/audiences; improve landing page/CTA based on real data.

Month 3: scale what is producing qualified booked calls and compare customer acquisition cost against setup fees + monthly recurring revenue.

Do not judge only by impressions/clicks.

Track:

  • Spend.
  • CPC.
  • Click-through rate.
  • Landing page conversion rate.
  • Cost per lead.
  • Cost per booked audit.
  • Show-up rate.
  • Qualified lead rate.
  • Demo-to-close rate.
  • Cost per acquired customer.
  • MRR generated.
  • Setup fees generated.

Early Benchmark Logic

If Page Pros gets cheap leads but they are not business owners, the campaign is failing.

If leads are expensive but qualified and book audits, the campaign may be working.

If traffic clicks but does not book, landing page/offer needs work.

If booked calls happen but no closes, sales script or qualification needs work.
