# 1APP Associate Sales Program — Version 3

**Supersedes:** the previous fixed-plan selling model.  
**Commercial model:** custom quote by automation.  
**Legal note:** independent-contractor classification and commission language require Ontario legal/accounting review before agreements are issued.

## 1. Purpose

Associates create qualified opportunities without creating bad promises for fulfillment. 1APP owns the brand, client, pricing catalogue, quote approval, account, implementation, and final decision on every deal.

## 2. Minimum entry requirements

Every associate must have:

- Reliable laptop or desktop; a phone alone is not enough.
- Stable internet, webcam/headset, current browser, and MFA capability.
- General computer skill: email, calendars, forms, spreadsheets, uploads, video calls, and screen sharing.
- Basic AI literacy: write a clear prompt, verify output, recognize hallucinations, and protect sensitive data.
- Ability to guide a client through the onboarding checklist.
- Professional communication and willingness to use the 1APP system of record.

Automatic disqualification:

- No suitable computer/internet.
- Cannot complete basic screen-share tasks.
- Exaggerates capabilities or income.
- Refuses CRM/process discipline.
- Mishandles passwords or client information.

## 3. Levels and authority

### Level 1 — Referral Associate

**Role:** warm introduction and demo booking only.

May:

- Identify a qualified business owner.
- Make a warm introduction.
- Submit the lead and book an approved demo.
- Share approved 1APP material.

May not:

- Run independent discovery/demos unless approved.
- Prepare or present a custom quote.
- Promise price, scope, integration, timeline, or result.

**Recommended quote-based compensation:** 10% of the collected setup fee, minimum $50 and maximum $150, paid once. No recurring commission. This replaces plan-name referral bonuses and requires owner/legal approval.

### Level 2 — Sales Associate

**Role:** prospect, qualify, run approved discovery, request a quote, follow up, and help close.

May:

- Use approved discovery scripts and demo assets.
- Select catalogue items in a **draft quote request**.
- Present an owner-approved quote.
- Complete the onboarding/handoff template.

May not:

- Set or discount prices independently.
- Approve technical feasibility.
- Promise launch dates or custom integrations.
- Modify legal/commercial terms.

**Current recommended compensation, subject to signed agreement:**

- 15% of net collected setup fees.
- 20% of net collected monthly recurring service revenue.
- Recurring cap: 12 paid months per client.
- Collected revenue only; excludes taxes, usage pass-throughs, refunds, credits, chargebacks, and third-party costs.

### Level 3 — Certified 1APP Partner

**Role:** proven associate who can close cleanly, complete onboarding, protect retention, and coach approved associates.

Requirements:

- Complete training and certification.
- At least three paid-active clients unless owners waive in writing.
- Clean CRM and handoff history.
- No material brand/compliance violations.
- Written approval from 1APP leadership.

**Current recommended compensation, subject to signed agreement:**

- 20% of net collected setup fees.
- 20% of net collected monthly recurring service revenue while the client and partner remain eligible under the agreement.
- Team rights/overrides only when activated in writing.

### Optional direct-team override

- 5% of net collected setup fees and 5% of net collected monthly recurring service revenue from direct recruits only.
- One level deep; no stacking on the partner's own deal.
- Recruiting associate must train, monitor, and correct the recruit.
- No income claims.
- Rights may be paused or removed for conduct, inactivity, or quality issues.

## 4. What associates sell

1APP sells custom automation solutions, not locked tiers.

- Public starting point: as low as $99/month.
- Revenue Reactivation Sprint for eligible businesses: no upfront setup fee, disclosed messaging usage, and a tiered/capped success fee tied to the written tracked result.
- Sprint launch requires an approved dormant cohort, lawful-contact attestation, campaign ID, success event, attribution window, outcome owner, and reconciliation method.
- Advanced/custom work requires a paid setup project and separate delivery schedule.
- Final quote depends on automations, setup, usage, integrations, support, and complexity.
- Bundle recommendations are starting solutions—not rigid plans.

Associates diagnose first, then request pricing. They do not quote from memory.

## 5. Quote workflow

1. Complete discovery and identify the measurable problem.
2. Choose the smallest useful automation set.
3. Create a draft in the approved quote builder.
4. State every dependency, assumption, exclusion, and client responsibility.
5. Submit to Tony for technical scope/time approval.
6. Submit to Justin for commercial/discount approval.
7. Present only the approved quote version.
8. Record sent date, follow-up date, objections, and next action.
9. On acceptance, complete the onboarding/technical handoff.
10. Mark the quote **Revenue Reactivation Sprint** or **Paid Implementation Required**.
11. Sprint outbound timing begins only after the approved cohort, compliance attestation, message, tracking, and test pass are complete.
12. Never promise a Sprint launch slot until Tony confirms capacity; the initial limit is four live Sprints and two new launches per week.

## 6. Lead attribution and ownership

A lead is protected only when it is:

- A real business owner/decision-maker.
- Properly created in 1APP before another source.
- Assigned to the associate.
- Supported by meaningful logged activity and a dated next action.

Standard protection window: 90 days. 1APP may reassign after 30 days without meaningful activity. Duplicate or disputed attribution is decided by 1APP records and owner judgment.

## 7. Sales process

1. **Identify:** target active service businesses with lead/booking/follow-up problems.
2. **Connect:** use approved outreach and record the response.
3. **Qualify:** confirm decision-maker, real pain, timeline, and ability to evaluate/pay.
4. **Audit/demo:** map the current process and show only relevant automations.
5. **Draft quote:** automation line items or approved solution bundle.
6. **Approval:** Tony technical; Justin commercial.
7. **Present and follow up:** no unapproved concessions.
8. **Handoff:** complete the technical onboarding template.
9. **Trial:** manage checkpoints and evidence during 14 days.
10. **Convert:** paid-active only after authorization and cleared payment.

## 8. Required certification

Before selling independently, an associate must pass:

- 1APP product and customer-fit test.
- Computer/AI practical test.
- Discovery role-play.
- Quote-request exercise.
- Onboarding/handoff exercise.
- CRM navigation and data-security test.
- Brand/claims test.

Training-account access comes first. Live access is limited to assigned records after certification.

## 9. Non-negotiable conduct

- Say 1APP only; never expose internal vendors or underlying platforms.
- Never guarantee leads, revenue, rankings, response rates, or savings.
- Never invent a feature, integration, price, discount, or launch date.
- Never collect client payment personally without written authority.
- Never put credentials or sensitive data in forms, notes, or unapproved AI tools.
- Use approved consent, opt-out, and privacy processes.
- Every opportunity must have one owner and one dated next action.

## 10. Commission control

- Commission is earned only as defined in the signed level-specific agreement.
- Payments are based on net collected eligible revenue.
- Refunds, credits, disputes, or chargebacks may reverse related commission.
- Quote acceptance, trial start, or an unpaid invoice alone does not create recurring commission.
- No commission applies to an unauthorized price, promise, or scope.

## 11. Program scorecard

Track weekly:

- New qualified opportunities.
- First conversations.
- Demos booked/held.
- Quote requests submitted/approved/sent.
- Quote acceptance rate: accepted quotes ÷ decision-ready quotes.
- Sprint launches/results reviews.
- Sprint-to-paid-expansion rate: clients buying broader automation ÷ Sprints that reached the results review.
- Paid-active clients and MRR.
- Handoff rejection/missing-information rate.
- 30/60/90-day retention.
- Conduct or compliance exceptions.
