# Dot vs. 1APP — Rod Norville Objection Card

## Core Positioning

Do not attack or dismiss Dot. A prospect already using an AI agent understands the value of automation.

**Dot is primarily a general, always-on work agent.** It can work across connected tools and projects, research, analyze data, prepare documents, use a cloud browser/computer, and communicate through supported channels and plugins.

**1APP is a customer-facing lead, communication, booking, and revenue system for trades and service businesses.** It can capture inquiries, recover missed calls, run SMS/email follow-up, organize contacts and opportunities in a CRM pipeline, book appointments, send reminders, request reviews, and run approved reactivation campaigns.

The positioning is:

> Dot can keep handling internal admin. 1APP can handle the customer-facing revenue workflow around calls, leads, estimates, bookings, follow-up, and reviews.

## Best First Reply

Send as two short messages rather than one long paragraph.

**Message 1**

> That’s good—Dot can keep handling your internal admin. Does it also text missed callers, follow up old quotes, and track every lead through booking?

**If the answer is no, partly, or uncertain**

> That’s where 1APP may fit. We handle the customer-facing lead and follow-up system without replacing what already works. Early morning or end of day better for Rod?

## Softer Discovery Version

> Makes sense. What does Dot currently handle for you—email and paperwork, or customer calls, texts, booking, estimates, and follow-up too?

Then respond to the gap they identify. Do not list every 1APP feature unless asked.

## Revenue Reactivation Angle

If the prospect has old leads, past customers, or unfinished quotes:

> Does Dot actively work your approved old leads and quotes, handle the replies, and turn qualified interest into tracked estimate appointments?

If not:

> That’s the gap our 14-Day Revenue Reactivation Sprint is designed to test. Dot can keep doing your admin while 1APP works the approved contact list.

Do not guarantee results or confirm eligibility before qualification.

## If They Ask Whether Dot and 1APP Can Connect

> Possibly. We first check what Dot is connected to now. Shared email, calendar, Slack or Teams, supported plugins, APIs, webhooks, or an automation bridge may provide a handoff. Our technical team confirms the exact setup before promising an integration.

Never promise a direct Dot-to-1APP integration without a technical review. Ask which Dot product/account they use, what tools are connected, and what information must pass between systems.

## Discovery Questions

Ask one at a time:

1. What does Dot currently handle for you?
2. Does it answer customer calls or text missed callers immediately?
3. Does it follow up estimates and old leads automatically?
4. Does every inquiry enter a visible sales pipeline with an owner and next step?
5. Does it book appointments, send reminders, and request reviews?
6. Where does the process still need you to step in manually?

## Do Not Say

- “1APP is better than Dot.”
- “Dot cannot do that” unless the prospect confirms it or the exact product is verified.
- “We integrate directly with Dot” without technical validation.
- “You should replace Dot.”

## Internal Comparison

**Dot is strongest for:** ongoing knowledge work, research, documents, data analysis, software tasks, browser work, and coordinating work across supported tools.

**1APP is strongest for:** business phone/SMS/email communication, missed-call recovery, lead capture, CRM pipelines, calendars, appointment workflows, reminders, review requests, customer follow-up, and approved dormant-contact reactivation.

**Overlap:** tasks, follow-up, automations, connected tools, and reducing manual admin.

**Best combined model:** Dot helps the owner manage internal work; 1APP runs and records the repeatable customer journey from inquiry to booked opportunity and follow-up.
