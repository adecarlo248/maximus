Rod's 1APP Sales Manager Training Module

Goal: Rod can explain 1APP simply, diagnose the client's operational leak, prepare an approved quote, onboard the client, and hand Tony a build-ready file without guessing.

What Rod is selling

1APP is a managed automation and follow-up system for service businesses. Clients are not buying a pile of software features. They are buying a configured operating process that helps them:

  • Respond to leads faster.
  • Recover missed calls.
  • Book and remind appointments.
  • Follow up on inquiries and estimates.
  • Organize leads and next actions.
  • Request reviews consistently.
  • Reduce repetitive administrative work.
  • See what is happening in their pipeline.

Current offer

  • Custom quote built from selected automations.
  • Starts as low as $99/month.
  • Revenue Reactivation Sprint: no upfront setup fee, disclosed messaging usage, and a tiered/capped success fee tied to the written tracked result.
  • One-time setup, monthly recurring service, usage/pass-through charges, and optional/custom work are quoted separately. HST is shown separately and is not a fifth price category.
  • The smallest useful solution should be recommended first.

Revenue Reactivation Sprint rules

  • The Sprint uses only an approved dormant lead/past-customer cohort; active opportunities and current estimates are excluded.
  • The client must confirm a lawful contact basis in writing. Identification, opt-out, suppression, and stop rules are mandatory.
  • The outbound campaign runs for 14 days after the approved list, message, tracking, and test pass are complete.
  • The fee is selected from the approved job-value tier and includes a hard cap per attributed sale.
  • Percentage fees use collected revenue or disclosed gross profit—not quoted contract value, verbal commitments, or unpaid invoices.
  • Every Sprint records the success event, campaign ID, attribution window, outcome owner, reconciliation source, cancellations, and refunds.
  • Initial capacity is limited to four live Sprints and two new Sprint launches per week unless Tony approves a written exception.

Rod's boundaries

Rod may:

  • Prospect, qualify, demonstrate, follow up, and manage the sales team.
  • Prepare quotes using approved catalogue items and bundle rules.
  • Collect non-secret onboarding requirements.
  • Track leads, opportunities, quotes, trial dates, tasks, and attribution.
  • Guide the client through approved onboarding steps.

Rod may not:

  • Promise technical feasibility, results, or launch dates without Tony's approval.
  • Invent pricing, discounts, integrations, or custom deliverables.
  • Publish or change live client automations.
  • Collect passwords, payment details, API keys, or verification codes in forms, notes, email, or chat.
  • Give associates administrator access.
  • Quote a Sprint without the approved tier, fee basis, cap, attribution, and compliance controls.
  • Promise a Sprint slot or start date before Tony accepts the handoff and confirms capacity.

Product-belief principle

Rod is not expected to believe in 1APP because of a feature list. He should believe in it because he can explain, measure, and defend the business change each automation is designed to create.

For every automation, Rod must know:

  1. The symptom the owner notices.
  2. The root process that is breaking.
  3. The exact step 1APP changes.
  4. Which of the five approved value categories applies: money saved, capacity recovered, revenue influenced, cash-flow improvement, or risk/visibility value.
  5. The baseline and outcome metric used to prove it.
  6. The variables outside 1APP's control.
  7. The safe claim and the prohibited claim.

Rod must use 08-Automation-Value-and-ROI-Field-Guide.md during discovery practice, quote preparation, trial design, and certification.

Eight-session training plan

Each session is 60–90 minutes. Rod must complete the exercise and pass the checkpoint before advancing.

Session 1 — The 1APP promise and ideal client

1APP helps service businesses stop losing opportunities to missed calls, slow follow-up, forgotten estimates, no-shows, and scattered customer communication. We identify the leak and install the smallest useful automation system around the way the business already operates.

  • Best-fit clients: active trades, contractors, local services, clinics, and appointment/estimate-driven businesses.
  • Poor fits: no operating business, no decision-maker, guaranteed-results demands, primarily custom software requests, or refusal to provide setup information.

Session 3 — Discovery and qualification

Use SPIN:

Situation

  • How do leads arrive today?
  • Who responds, and in what tools?

Problem

  • What happens when a call is missed?
  • Where do booking, estimates, follow-up, reviews, or payments break down?

Implication

  • How many opportunities or staff hours may be lost each week?
  • What happens when the owner is on a job and cannot respond?
  • What is the average collected job value, close rate, no-show rate, response time, or loaded staff cost relevant to this problem?

Need-payoff

  • If that step happened automatically and the team could see the next action, what would improve?

Required qualification

  • Real operating business.
  • Decision-maker involved or accessible.
  • One measurable operational problem.
  • Willingness to complete onboarding, approve the dormant cohort, and report outcomes during the Sprint and attribution window.
  • Use case 1APP can deliver reliably.
  • A usable dormant list or a willingness to approve a paid setup project instead.

Exercise: run a 15-minute mock discovery, write a one-sentence problem statement, record the current baseline, and show the business-impact math with assumptions labelled.

Objection responses

“It costs too much.”

Before we compare the price, which part feels out of line—the setup work, the monthly service, or whether the problem is expensive enough to solve? We should compare the quote with the cost of missed opportunities and staff time, not with software the business must build itself.

“We already have software.”

That may be fine. What do you wish the current setup did more consistently? If there is no meaningful gap, I would not recommend replacing it.

“Can you guarantee more jobs?”

No responsible provider can guarantee lead volume or revenue. We can build and test the response, booking, follow-up, and tracking process so fewer existing opportunities are lost.

“Can you integrate with this tool?”

I will document the exact system and workflow and have Tony confirm feasibility, scope, and timing before we promise it.

Final certification standard

Rod is ready when he can answer seven questions for every deal:

  1. What business problem are we solving?
  2. Which exact automation solves it?
  3. Which of the five approved value categories applies, and how does the automation create that value?
  4. Which client-supplied baseline and success metric will prove or disprove the value?
  5. Which assumptions and variables are outside 1APP's control?
  6. What does the client have to provide?
  7. What must Tony build, test, and approve?
