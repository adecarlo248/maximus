This pack replaces plan-based selling with 1APP's current quote-based model:

- Custom automations selected after discovery.
- Public pricing starts as low as **$99/month**.
- **1APP Revenue Reactivation Sprint** (Document 10) — the primary low-friction offer for businesses with a usable dormant lead/customer list. No upfront setup fee; messaging usage is disclosed and 1APP earns a tiered, capped success fee only when the written tracked result occurs.
- **Paid starter automation** — the alternative for businesses without a suitable dormant list. Scope, setup, monthly service, usage, and delivery are quoted normally.
- Advanced, custom, integration-heavy, migration, website, and multi-location work requires a paid setup project and separate delivery schedule.
- One-time setup fees and monthly service fees are quoted separately.
- Final scope, price, delivery estimate, usage, client responsibilities, and exclusions must be approved in writing.

## Control rules

- These prices are an **internal starting framework**, not automatic public commitments.
- Rod and associates may prepare a quote from the approved catalogue. Tony approves technical feasibility and delivery. Justin approves commercial terms. Non-standard discounts or scope require both owners.
- A delivery estimate begins only after the quote is accepted, the handoff is complete, required access/content is received, and Tony accepts the work into the build queue.
- The Sprint's 14-day outbound window begins only after the approved cohort, written lawful-contact attestation, approved copy, campaign ID, stop logic, and test pass are complete.
- Client-facing language says **1APP** only.
- No one may promise results, revenue, lead volume, rankings, or an unapproved integration.
- Every recommendation must include a documented baseline, financial mechanism, primary success measure, and variables outside 1APP's control.
- The five approved value categories are money saved, capacity recovered, revenue influenced, cash-flow improvement, and risk/visibility value.
- The four commercial price categories are one-time setup, monthly recurring service, usage/pass-through charges, and optional/custom work. HST or other tax is shown separately and is not a fifth price category.
- Performance-fee, privacy, CASL, and attribution terms require owner approval and Ontario legal review before client use.
