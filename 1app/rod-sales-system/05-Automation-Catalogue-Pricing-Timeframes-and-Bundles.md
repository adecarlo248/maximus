# 1APP Internal Automation Catalogue, Pricing, Timeframes & Bundles

**Status:** Internal working price book — owner approval required before client use.  
**Currency:** CAD before HST.  
**Offer:** Custom quote or eligible Revenue Reactivation Sprint. The Sprint has no upfront setup fee; messaging usage and tiered/capped performance terms are disclosed in writing.  
**Public claim:** Starting as low as $99/month.

## Pricing logic

Each quote may contain:

1. **One-time setup:** discovery validation, configuration, copy, connection, testing, training, and launch.
2. **Monthly service:** platform access, monitoring, support, reporting, and ongoing operation.
3. **Usage/pass-through:** phone, SMS, email, AI, payment, or third-party charges where applicable.
4. **Custom work:** non-standard integrations, migration, development, or complex data work.

### Delivery-clock rule

Timeframes are estimates in business days. The clock starts only after:

- Accepted quote and scope.
- Complete handoff.
- Required client content and secure access.
- Tony's technical acceptance and build-queue start.

The clock pauses for client delay, third-party approval, porting, compliance review, unavailable APIs, scope change, or failed client testing.

## Bundle and discount rules

- Bundle savings apply only while the listed components remain in scope.
- No extra discount without Tony and Justin's written approval.
- Never discount usage/pass-through costs.
- Never offer a discount merely because a prospect asks. Exchange it for reduced scope, longer commitment, annual prepayment, case-study rights, or a faster decision—when approved.
- Maximum standard owner-approved discount: 10% from the relevant line-item or bundle price.
- More than 10% requires written exception with reason, expiry, and value received.
- Setup and monthly fees must never be blended into one vague number.

## 14-day trial rules

- The Revenue Reactivation Sprint replaces the generic free trial. It is a 14-day outbound campaign to an approved dormant cohort with no upfront setup fee, disclosed messaging usage, and a written tiered/capped success fee.
- The outbound window begins only after the approved cohort, lawful-contact attestation, approved copy, campaign ID, stop logic, booking/handoff path, outcome owner, and test pass are complete.
- Billable outcomes, attribution windows, fee basis, hard cap per sale, refund/cancellation treatment, and reconciliation method must be written before launch.
- Fees apply only to the agreed tracked result; quoted value, verbal commitments, and uncollected contract value are not billable.
- Full-system automation, custom integrations/APIs, accounting/payment connections, migrations, custom pages, and expanded workflows are paid implementations with separate scope and delivery schedules.
- See Document 10 for niche fit, pricing tiers, scripts, eligibility, and compliance controls.

## Quote checklist

- [ ] Client problem stated in the client's words.
- [ ] Selected automations map directly to that problem.
- [ ] Relevant baseline is recorded using client-provided or verified data.
- [ ] Financial mechanism is identified: money saved, capacity recovered, revenue influenced, cash-flow improvement, or risk/visibility value.
- [ ] Value scenario shows inputs, formula, assumptions, and limitations; it is not presented as a guarantee.
- [ ] One primary success measure and supporting measures are agreed.
- [ ] Variables outside 1APP's control are documented.
- [ ] The four commercial price categories are separate: one-time setup, monthly recurring service, usage/pass-through charges, and optional/custom work. HST is shown separately and is not a fifth price category.
- [ ] Bundle savings are accurate.
- [ ] Delivery clock and dependencies are stated.
- [ ] Trial scope, dates, success measures, and conversion price are stated.
- [ ] Offer path is marked **Revenue Reactivation Sprint** or **Paid Implementation Required**.
- [ ] Sprint quote includes the approved cohort, job-value tier, fee basis/rate/cap, success event, attribution window, campaign ID, outcome owner, reconciliation source, and compliance status.
- [ ] Exclusions and client responsibilities are stated.
- [ ] Tony approved feasibility/timeframe.
- [ ] Justin approved commercial terms/discount.
- [ ] Quote ID/version and expiry date recorded.
