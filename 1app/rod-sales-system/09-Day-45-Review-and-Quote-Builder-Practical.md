# Day-45 Performance Review & Quote-Builder Practical

## Purpose

This document defines Rod's Day-45 performance review and makes the quote-builder certification practical executable from the master PDF, the live browser tool, or the editable ZIP.

## Day-45 performance review

**Timing:** Day 45 after Rod's Effective Date.  
**Participants:** Rod, Tony, and Justin.  
**Record owner:** Rod prepares the data; Tony and Justin validate their areas and sign the action plan.

### Required conversion rates

1. **Quote-acceptance rate**

> Accepted quotes ÷ decision-ready quotes × 100

A decision-ready quote is an owner-approved quote issued to the decision-maker with complete scope, price, and next action. Drafts, duplicates, withdrawn technical requests, and quotes still awaiting owner approval are excluded.

2. **Sprint-to-paid-expansion rate**

> Clients buying broader paid automation ÷ Sprints that reached the results review × 100

A Sprint reaches results review when its 14-day outbound window is complete and the agreed reporting checkpoint has occurred. Active Sprints that have not reached that checkpoint are excluded. A paid expansion requires written authorization for broader automation and the first required cleared payment.

### Review agenda

1. Validate both rates using actual counts and source reports.
2. Review pipeline coverage, quote turnaround, follow-up completion, and loss reasons.
3. Review handoff rejection or rework, trial capacity, implementation load, and first-30-day support demand.
4. Identify one sales behaviour, one handoff behaviour, and one operating constraint to improve.
5. Assign each action an owner and deadline for the next 45 days.

### Decision rules

- Do not compare percentages without checking the denominators.
- Do not count open quotes as rejected or active trials as failed.
- Do not change targets, commission, scope authority, or promotion rules verbally. Any change requires a signed written amendment.
- The Day-45 performance review is separate from the Day-45 user-access review.
